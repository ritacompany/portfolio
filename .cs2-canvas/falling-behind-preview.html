Falling behind

Every Action Item in a week is due by Saturday night. Some weeks, one of them is still open when that night comes, and it has to go somewhere.

Push forward

Everything left lands in next week, which gets heavier right after a hard one.

Let them pick

They decide what to keep at the moment they are behind.

Ruled out

Drop it

The leftover disappears and the Pathway slips without anyone seeing it.

Kept

Carry over

The week stays the size of their capacity.

A week that comes up short can feel like failing, and that feeling makes people less likely to keep going. So anything left over moves into the next week on its own, and that week takes fewer new Action Items to stay within their capacity.
