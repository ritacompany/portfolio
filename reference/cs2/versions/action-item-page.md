## Placement

Recommendation: move the block to directly after the Action Hub and before Calendar setup.

What the frame holds (frame 4269:9433, read 5 Nov): the Action Item page already exists as a placeholder at the
very bottom, eyebrow Action Item page, then the word Placeholder, after Start of week. The order above it is
Hero, Background, Competitive analysis, The foundation, Growth Journey, Pathway, Action hub, Calendar setup,
Schedule view, Scheduled times, Personalized timeline, Buffer flow, Falling behind, Start of week.

Why after the Action Hub:
1. The Action Hub is where a person meets an Action Item, and its popover offers go to the Action Item, mark
   complete and reschedule (outline block 6). The page is the destination of the first of those, so the reader
   sees the destination right after the entry point.
2. Everything after the hub then reads as a tool around a thing the reader has already seen: the calendar
   places Action Items, buffer flow pulls more of them forward, Falling behind moves one that is left over,
   Start of week lists them. Right now the reader meets all of that without ever seeing what an Action Item is.
3. It follows the descent the page already makes: Growth Journey, Pathway, Action Hub, Action Item. Four screens
   in a row, each one level closer to the work.
4. The end of the page is better as Start of week plus the new ending. Start of week closes the week story
   (the recap, rollover, recalibration, reset) and the ending ties it to the weekly hours. A screen about the
   work itself after those would reset the story to the start.

What the block must not repeat from the page: only the Active item can be checked off is already said in the
Action Hub block (so here it is one clause, not a point); reschedule is explained in Scheduled times, so here it
is only named; target, pace and capacity are not mentioned.

What would change it: if the finished media (a short video) is a full walk through of finishing one Action
Item, it works as a payoff and the page could end there, with the ending block after it. If the reschedule
mention before Scheduled times feels early once he reads it in place, move it after Scheduled times and keep
the order Hub, Calendar setup, Schedule view, Scheduled times, Action Item page. His outline note that the page
ends on the Action Item page (2 Nov) was before Falling behind and Start of week were written; the sibling
session's ending in intro-and-ending.md assumes the page ends on Start of week, so it needs no change if the
block moves up.

## Version A: the AI is the writer

Covers: AI writes the content, about ten block types, plain on purpose, text only at launch, mark complete only
on Active.
Eyebrow: Action Item
Headline: No one writes an Action Item by hand, so its page has to hold whatever the AI puts in it and still read well.
Body: I didn't design what these pages say, because the AI writes it. I designed about ten block types for that writing to land in, like a definition or a do and do not comparison. They stay plain on purpose, since a block shaped like a feature would limit what the AI can say. At launch each Action Item is text only, and only the Active one can be marked complete.

Supersedes round 2 (its "I kept it as simple as I could" read as if he ran out of ideas). His point: the AI that
writes the Action Items was still being built, so the page had to be built to be filled by an AI whose details
were not known yet, and the business scope for the MVP was small. He has plenty of ideas for it. The block
therefore carries the constraint as the reason, then the room to grow. One version. Humanizer run as the skill on
this round after he asked (the first pass was from memory); two edits: send-off ending and slogan closer
cut. Dash scan clean. Not reader checked, no canvas.

Where it traces (reasoning/action-item-page.md): curriculum output only, no AI surface on the page, so the AI is
entirely upstream and the page has to be legible with no interaction (section 2, his 2 May 2026); structure
kept loose in case the AI could not use it initially because of its script (section 2, same entry); the page is
a set of containers the AI chooses between and not features (section 1, his 13 May 2026); read only and text
only, two MVP scoping calls (section 1). The "AI still being built" and "the business required it" framing is
his own, said in chat on 5 Nov and not in the record, so it needs his confirmation before it ships.

Eyebrow: Action Item
Headline: Someone has to read each piece of work the AI writes, and when I designed the page the AI was still being built.

So the page is a set of plain containers the AI can fill however it turns out to work, and I kept the structure loose in case it couldn't use all of it at first. That keeps the page simple for the MVP and leaves room to build it out once the AI is ready.
