# Section 7: The Action Hub and the Growth Journey

## Block 1

Eyebrow: Before a career is chosen

Headline: An empty Action Hub invites the quiz.

Someone who has not taken the quiz opens an Action Hub with nothing in it yet, and an invitation to take the quiz and be matched with tech careers. A page that says it is still being built feels dishonest, as if that part of the product does not exist.

## Block 2

Eyebrow: Home base

Headline: The week is a list with times and a target.

The list view shows this week's Action Items with suggested times and weekly progress. The Pathway target and pace sit in the rail beside it, because they are the only anchor the weekly adjustments have. Each card shows its scheduled time, while the schedule view shows where the items sit among the week's events.

## Block 3

Eyebrow: No calendar

Headline: The list still works without a calendar.

Without a calendar, the same list shows a Schedule smarter prompt that offers to connect one. The view stays visible instead of disappearing. The idea for a week too packed to schedule was a plain list with no specific times, everything done by the end of the week.

## Block 4

Eyebrow: First time

Headline: A short tip explains the scheduled time.

An Action Item's time slot carries a tip that says Mondai picked the time from the person's availability, and that the icon reschedules it anytime. Tips appear only where they earn their place, and the first visit has to feel like a real start.

## Block 5

Eyebrow: The long view

Headline: The Growth Journey page holds the bigger goal.

The page shows a personalized summary, the journey target, journey pace, progress and the five Pathways. It holds the journey numbers because carrying both levels in one place was confusing. Capacity can be changed from here, since capacity moves the journey numbers.

# Section 8: Calendar integration

## Block 1

Eyebrow: First week

Headline: Calendar integration is offered, not forced.

In week one a person chooses to connect a calendar or manage their own schedule. The calendar step is written to represent a decision, with no forceful integration messaging.

## Block 2

Eyebrow: Boundaries

Headline: After sync, the person sets the window.

Once the calendar is synced, the person sets a preferred time frame and their sharpest hours, and Mondai suggests a range. The preferred times depend on the window they set, and the time bar version is the one that was picked.

## Block 3

Eyebrow: Moving things

Headline: A scheduled time is a suggestion that can be moved.

The schedule view lets a person move an Action Item to a new time. Scheduled times are recommendations and assistance, not due dates, because completion is decided at the end of the week. Mondai does not reschedule on its own, since that could get confusing fast.

## Block 4

Eyebrow: Edge cases

Headline: A packed calendar falls back to the plain list.

When the calendar is too full, a notice says it is packed this week and the scheduled times are removed. The idea was to switch that week to the non calendar approach, since a manual flow could be a lot of work for a fringe case. When a sync fails, a module over the list says the calendar lacks available times or could not sync.

# Section 11: Action Items

## Block 1

Eyebrow: Two pages

Headline: The Pathway page lists one Pathway at a time.

The Growth Journey stays the overview, and the Pathway page is where someone learns a single Pathway. It lists that Pathway's Focus Areas and Action Items, with breadcrumbs back to the journey page.

## Block 2

Eyebrow: Sequence

Headline: Focus Areas run in order, so Action Items do too.

Focus Areas turned out to be linear, so the Action Items progress through them in order instead of sitting under a sorting tag. They are numbered on the page, a rail marks each one Complete, Current or Not started, and the Action Items in later Focus Areas are locked.
