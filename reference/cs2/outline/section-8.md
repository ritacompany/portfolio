# Block plan: section 8, Calendar integration

## 8. Calendar integration
Frame block: proposed
Reader gets: How the week fits real calendars.
Flows: C1, C2, D3, D4, D5

### Block 1: Offered, not forced
Reader has to see: The first week choice to connect a calendar or manage your own schedule.
Flows: C1
Claim: product | Week 1 offers a choice to connect a calendar or manage your own schedule | src: 2941:13664, 4901:28506
Claim: decision | He asked that the calendar step represent a decision and not use forceful integration messaging | src: dossier:calendar#Q4.1

### Block 2: Setting the boundaries
Reader has to see: The preferred time frame and sharpest hours the user sets after sync, with a range Mondai suggests.
Flows: C2
Claim: product | After sync the user sets a preferred time frame and sharpest hours and Mondai suggests a range | src: 2941:13708, 4901:28550
Claim: decision | He picked the time bar version, especially for preferred time | src: dossier:calendar#Q4.4
Claim: decision | Preferred times must depend on the window the user sets | src: dossier:calendar#Q4.5

### Block 3: Rescheduling an Action Item
Reader has to see: The schedule view where a user moves an Action Item to a new time, and what the times mean.
Flows: D3
Claim: product | The schedule view lets the user reschedule an Action Item to a new time | src: 2882:34507, 2882:34590
Claim: decision | Scheduled times are recommendations and assistance tools, not due dates, because completion is decided at the end of the week | src: dossier:calendar#Q4.9
Claim: decision | There is no auto reschedule because it could get confusing fast in the product | src: dossier:calendar#Q4.10

### Block 4: When the calendar cannot hold the week
Reader has to see: The packed calendar notice and the sync failure screen.
Flows: D4, D5
Claim: product | When the calendar is too full Mondai removes scheduled times and says the calendar is packed | src: 2616:14229, 2616:14541
Claim: product | A calendar sync failure has its own screen | src: 2693:9409
Claim: decision | For a packed calendar he proposed switching the user to the non calendar approach for that week, because a manual flow could be a lot of work for a fringe case | src: dossier:calendar#Q4.7
