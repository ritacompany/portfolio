## Not gaps. In the files, and I should have used them.

The archive carries the real argument for almost every mechanic, including the alternatives
and what breaks. Where the case study currently paraphrases thinly, that is my failure to
use what is there.

- **Five pathways, why sequential.** Full argument, pathway by pathway. "You can't build in
  a direction you haven't picked... networking without artifacts to point at reads as
  fishing... warm outreach outperforms cold at this audience's odds."
- **Focus Areas screenless.** Reasoning plus the mental-model consequence plus the AI
  scheduling consequence.
- **Capacity, declared over observed.** Two-to-three week cold start. That was the real
  reason, not my reconstruction.
- **Target stable, not locked.** Including the one-week delay on capacity changes as an
  anti-escape-hatch, which I never used and which is the sharpest detail in the section.
- **Pace weekly, and "Pace" over "Projected".** Capacity is a velocity input, pace is a
  velocity output, and the vocabulary link is what makes it teachable.
- **Cognitive load per item.** Resolves a real paradox: a hard weekly cap is violated the
  moment rollover exists.
- **Rollover silent.** The killed modal and the reasoning.
- **Five states, why five.** What is lost by collapsing each pair, plus presence versus
  absence as the tiebreaker.
- **Past-time dimming.** Precious app research on harmful language, Google Calendar 2011
  precedent.
- **Sequence gating, undo model, popover, within-week reschedule.** All reasoned.

**Also missed:** `04-WHATS-BROKEN-AND-MISSING.md` contains Chadwick's own verbatim rewrite

## Real gaps. Worth asking the original project for.

**1. Research to decision mapping.**
The archive names what each comparable product teaches. It does not record which
observation produced which Mondai decision. Chadwick's framing: "I learned from studying
______ that ______ creates a specific psychological response." That mapping does not exist
in writing anywhere in this folder, and it is the thing the case study most needs.

**2. UX strategy, observations and user psychology work.**
The archive has thirteen named behavioural principles attached to mechanics. It does not
have the strategy work: what was observed about these users, what was concluded, how those
conclusions shaped the product beyond the weekly framework.

**3. Personas and user research outputs.**
QN1 shows personas being requested and argued over. No record of what was produced or used.
