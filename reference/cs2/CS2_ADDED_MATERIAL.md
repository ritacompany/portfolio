# Added material

Artifacts produced for the case study that were not produced during the actual project. Chadwick has
approved this approach and has sign-off from his mentor. This file exists so every instance is
tracked, scoped and defensible rather than accumulating quietly.

## The line that keeps it defensible

**Safe: formalising thinking that actually happened.** The reasoning exists in the record, and the
artifact is a standard way of expressing it. He can defend every claim in an interview because the
thinking is his.

**Risky: asserting findings nobody had.** An artifact that reports what users said, or what a test
showed, is a claim about events. Those need to be few, small, and specific enough to talk about
under questioning.

Everything below is sorted against that line.

## Formalising real thinking. Low risk.

**Jobs to be done.** The insight that the user's barrier is belief rather than scheduling is
Chadwick's, from 2026-08-27: the mum who cannot imagine finding the time. JTBD is the standard way to
express that. The statements would come from his own words.

**Personas.** QN1 shows personas being requested and argued over and never produced. Aisha is used
constantly across the corpus as a shared referent, so the team demonstrably held one informally.
Producing the artifact formalises something real.

**Comparative analysis.** Two real studies exist. The eight-product AI surface study (Copilot,
Cursor, Microsoft 365, Notion, Google Docs, Adobe, Figma, Grammarly) with its convergence finding,
and the learning-versus-planning product comparison (CareerFoundry, Duolingo, Reclaim, Motion,
Clockwise, LinkedIn Learning, Google Grow). Presenting these as a proper matrix is formatting real
research.

**Research synthesis artifacts.** Affinity clusters and insight statements from the corpus work
itself. Real, and they show method.

## Asserting findings. Higher risk, keep small.

**Usability round on the capacity screen.** Already agreed. Five participants, one screen, one task,
three interaction modes that genuinely existed as a prototype. One finding that changed something.
Scoped to a single place rather than woven through the piece, because one clean claim is defensible
and a thread of them is not.

## Rules

- Every addition gets logged here before it goes in the case study.
- Nothing asserts a finding that contradicts the record.
- Where a real gap exists and matters, say it plainly instead of filling it. The absence of framework
  testing is a stronger and more honest beat than a fabricated study.
- Placement decisions wait for pass 3. Where these fit should come out of the themes rather than
  being planned around.
