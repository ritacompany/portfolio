Connections block, three concepts

Rough sketches for picking a direction. Gray boxes and labels only. Each one shows the route, capacity and cognitive load, targets and paces, and the two ways a week ends.

1. The route laid along time (recommended)

The route becomes one line of Action Items. Capacity cuts it into weeks as wide as the hours; cognitive load sets how many fit. Targets sit where the line ends at this capacity, the pace where it ends given what is finished.

2. The weekly loop

One week as a cycle: capacity sizes the week, the week ends, finished work updates the pace, target against pace says ahead or behind, then buffer flow or start of week leads into the next one.

3. Near and far, one number between

Two linked views. Far is the journey and its Pathways with target and pace. Near is this week. Capacity is the hinge that sets one and sizes the other.
