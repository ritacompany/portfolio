## Why the previous rounds failed

Each build mixed four layers into one artifact: foundation, story, copy and design. When it failed,
there was no way to tell which layer was broken, and no way to fix one without rebuilding all four.
Three rounds went that way.

The specific missing piece each time was the foundation. Nothing in any draft ever told a cold
reader what Mondai is, who uses it, or what they are trying to do, so every later sentence referred
to something that had never been established.

## The stages

Each is approved before the next starts.

**1. Foundation.** Four sentences in Chadwick's words, not paraphrased.
- What Mondai is, said plainly to someone who has never heard of it
- Who uses it and what they are actually trying to do
- What was wrong when he got there
- What this case study claims about him

Extracted by grilling rather than a questionnaire. Nothing is written until these are locked.

**2. Story shape.** Format chosen against the audience, then beats written as plain sentences. No
layout, no HTML. Readable in a minute.

**3. Copy.** Written as a document so the words are judged without design carrying them. Humanizer
over every line.

**4. Build.** Layout, diagrams, real screens, against approved copy.

## Skills to use, and where

- `grill-me` at stage 1
- `case-study` at stages 1 and 2
- `humanizer` at stage 3, on every line
- `information-architecture` and `user-flow-diagram` at stage 4
- `design-critique` as a self-review before publishing
- `artifact-design` at build
