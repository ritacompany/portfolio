# Stage 1 grill, prepared

Not a questionnaire. These are the openers, each with the follow-up that matters, so the session
moves rather than collecting answers.

## 1. What Mondai is

**Open:** Someone at a party asks what you're working on. They know nothing about careers in tech
and nothing about your company. What do you say.

**Follow up on:** any word that only means something inside Mondai. Journey, pathway, pace, target,
capacity, action item. If the plain version needs one of those, it isn't the plain version yet.

**Why it matters:** every failed opening referred to things this sentence was supposed to establish.

## 2. Who uses it

**Open:** Describe one real person who signs up. Not a persona. Someone you had in mind.

**Follow up on:** what their week actually looks like, and what they were doing before Mondai
existed. The claim that their weeks are unreliable needs to arrive as a fact about a person, not a
demographic statement.

**Watch for:** whether the answer is about who they are or about what they are trying to do. The
second is the useful one.

## 3. What they are trying to do

**Open:** What does success look like for that person, in their words, not the product's.

**Follow up on:** whether the product's date is the same thing as their goal. If it is not, the
target date needs explaining differently than any draft has explained it.

## 4. What was wrong when you got there

**Open:** What existed when you started, and what was missing.

**Follow up on:** whether the gap was a missing feature, a missing structure, or a missing decision.
He has said "the product was a set of features, the route between them had not been designed." That
is a strong line and it needs testing rather than reusing, since it came from the draft being
discarded.

## 5. What this case study claims about you

**Open:** A hiring manager reads this and comes away believing one thing about how you work. What is
it.

**Follow up on:** whether the claim needs the framework at all. It may be provable with less.

**Previous answer, worth testing rather than assuming:** that he understands how things connect,
what depends on what, and what it costs to move any one piece.

## 6. The one that decides the shape

**Open:** If a reader only reads the first screen and leaves, what do you need them to have taken
away.

**Why it matters:** this settles the format question without discussing formats.
