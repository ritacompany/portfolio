What eight people showed

Eight people typed the hours they could give each week, then logged what they really gave for two weeks. Six of the eight stayed within a fifth of their number.

Hours a week, typed and given. One row per person. Typed Gave, two week average

Before the first session I wrote down what would count as a fail: four or more of the eight giving under 70 percent of their number in both weeks. Two did. The other two tests held as well. Shown the two dates with no explanation, seven of eight predicted that an unfinished week moves the pace and leaves the target where it is. Six of eight read the Rollover screen as the plan making room for them; the other two read it as a mark against them.

The two who missed were the two who typed the most hours, and both gave under half of them by week two. Recalibration waits for three short weeks, so their plan would have stayed about twice as full as their real week for one more Sunday. I changed it to step in after two.

Two week diary and moderated prototype sessions, eight people recruited for the study, December 2026. Fail criteria written before the first session.
