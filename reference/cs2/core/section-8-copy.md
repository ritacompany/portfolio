## Block 1

Eyebrow: Your choice

Headline: Connecting a calendar is optional, and the week works without one.

In the first week, a person chooses between connecting a calendar and keeping the week as a list they fit in themselves. People run their lives very differently, and requiring a calendar would turn the week into a timetable with every hour set in advance, like school. Requiring one is also a push to schedule, and a study of 18,000 online learners found that pushing people to schedule did not help them stick with it (Baker, Evans and Dee 2016). Without a calendar, the week is the same list of Action Items, all due Saturday night, with an offer to connect one later.

## Block 2

Eyebrow: Finding time

Headline: The calendar finds free time, and the times it gives are suggestions.

With a calendar connected, Mondai places each Action Item in a gap between the person's own events. The week then shows as a calendar, the schedule view. Clicking an Action Item there opens the same item as it appears in the list view, with its suggested time shown on its card. An Action Item only has to be done by the end of the week, so a time on it is help, not a due date. Once a suggested time passes it drops off the card. Nothing is marked late, since what counts is whether the work is done by Saturday night.

## Block 3

Eyebrow: Setting limits

Headline: The person decides when Mondai may place work.

After connecting, the person sets a daily window, the hours Mondai is allowed to use, and inside it the hours they focus best. Focus hours have to sit inside the window, or Mondai would suggest times the person is not free. The window has to be at least eight hours, because the time Mondai can use is the window minus the person's own events, and a short window can be filled by events alone, leaving nowhere to put the work.

## Block 4

Eyebrow: Moving a time

Headline: Only the person moves a time.

Each Action Item card has a schedule button that opens a time picker, so the person can move it whenever they like. Mondai never reschedules on its own, because times that shift by themselves get confusing fast.

## Block 5

Eyebrow: A packed week

Headline: When the calendar is too full, the work goes back to the list.

Some weeks leave too few free gaps to place everything. When that happens, a notice says the calendar is packed this week. The Action Items that cannot be placed show no time, and the person works on them from the list, the same way as without a calendar. Falling back to the list keeps one way of using the week, even when the calendar cannot help.
