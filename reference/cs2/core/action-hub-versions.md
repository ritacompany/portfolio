## Version A: the screen first, three rail notes

Side text: It opens on this week's Action Items, the work picked for the week, and each one gets checked off here. Suggested times, the calendar and the start of every new week all happen on this screen. People come back to it most days, so it also carries the job of keeping them going.
Rail paragraph: The rail on the right shows how the current Pathway is going. So the rail tracks only the Pathway the person is in, and the whole journey stays one click away.

Note: Ahead of target | The gap between the two dates, in days. It is the only ahead or behind signal on the screen. There is no streak.

## Version B: what it is for, one paragraph on the dates
Eyebrow: The Action Hub
Headline: One screen for this week's work, and the screen people open most.
Side text: The Action Hub lists the Action Items for this week and nothing more. A person checks things off here, sees suggested times here and starts each new week here. Because they see it nearly every day, it does most of the work of keeping them going.
Rail paragraph: Over a long journey, people keep going when they can see a near goal. So the rail beside the list shows only the current Pathway.
How the dates move: The rail holds two dates. The target is when the Pathway should be done, set from the hours the person gives each week. The pace is where their progress actually points. Pace updates once a week, never with every finished item, and the gap between the two dates is the only ahead or behind signal. There is no streak.
