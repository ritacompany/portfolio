# Get ahead: what the frames and record hold (27 Oct)

Source frames: his Figma page VC, section Wireframes (4397:6311), text Get ahead flow and fifteen frames named Action hub weekly completion/STATE/Alt A to C, plus his snapshot How states are assigned (4622:12349).

## What it is
- Triggered when a person completes every Action Item for the week, whatever day that happens.
- The completion screen picks one of five states from two inputs read at that moment: streak (every required Action Item done in each previous week) and pace position (pace date against target date: ahead, on track within one day, behind by more than a day).
- MOMENTUM: streak active, ahead. CLEAN: streak inactive, ahead. STEADY: either, on track. TURNING: streak active, behind. RESET: streak inactive, behind.
- Each state has its own headline and body copy; steady offers a chance to get ahead, turning and reset a chance to catch up, by pulling next week's Action Items forward.
- Three copy alternates per state (Alt A to C).

## Decisions in the record

- Get ahead cannot sit only at the start of the week, because the days left to get ahead depend on how many days remain (line 16, his words, 6 May 2026).
