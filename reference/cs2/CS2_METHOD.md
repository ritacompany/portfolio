# CS2 method

Agreed 2026-08-27. This is the structure. It does not change without Chadwick saying so.

Supersedes the topic-by-topic approach in `reasoning/README.md`, which started from predefined
categories lifted off the archive's contents page and is the root cause of every failure since.

---

## The rule that was broken

From the `affinity-diagram` skill: *"Bottom-up clustering: group related data points into natural
clusters, do not start with predefined categories."*

The nine topic files started from categories (carry-over, capacity, states, pathways, calendar,
onboarding, AI surfaces, action item page, design system). Evidence was then gathered to fill them.
That produces a taxonomy of decisions, which has no time in it and cannot produce a story. It is
also why the narrative kept coming from Chadwick's voice memos instead of from the record.

The nine files are not discarded. They are the depth layer, correctly cited. They are not the spine.

---

## Pass 1. Extract

All 154 conversations, in order, none skipped.

From each: observations pulled verbatim, tagged with the conversation they came from. What he was
doing, asking, worried about, arguing with, changing his mind about. Who else was involved.

**No interpretation. No categories. No prose.** The output is a flat file of tagged notes. This is
capturing raw material, not composing.

## Pass 2. Cluster

Begins only when extraction is complete.

Group bottom-up. Cluster names come from the material.

**Coverage check, from the skill:** every conversation must appear somewhere in the clustered output.
If any is missing, go back for it. This is what prevents themes being built from the first few
sources and the rest fitted around them retroactively.

## Pass 3. Themes and insights

Three to five top-level themes. An insight statement for each. Frequency, intensity, and connections
between themes. Anything appearing in only one conversation is flagged as single-source rather than
dropped.

## Pass 4. Story

Format selected from the themes, using the `case-study` skill. First point at which anything is
written as prose. `humanizer` over all of it.

---

## Checkpoints

Chadwick reviews and can redirect at the end of every pass, before the next one starts. Passes are
also chunked internally so a wrong turn is caught inside a pass rather than at the end of it.

Rationale, in his words: one wrong decision early has repeatedly caused everything downstream to be
wrong, and by the time it surfaces the work has gone too far to recover.

## Standing rules

- `reference/PROTOCOL.md` runs before any question to Chadwick, and before any decision that
  might be his. It is the check that stops both failure modes.

- Any moment that could open or frame the case study is presented as options with what each costs.
  Rejected options are kept, not deleted, so we can fall back.
- Nothing is declared locked while questions are still open.
- Skills are searched for actively at each stage and proposed, not just named in passing.
