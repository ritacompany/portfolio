Section: 8

## Block 1

Eyebrow: First week

Headline: Calendar integration is offered, not forced.

In week one a person chooses to connect a calendar or manage their own schedule. The calendar step is written to represent a decision, with no forceful integration messaging.

## Block 2

Eyebrow: Boundaries

Headline: After sync, the person sets the window.

Once the calendar is synced, the person sets a preferred time frame and their sharpest hours, and Mondai suggests a range. The preferred times depend on the window they set, and the time bar version is the one that was picked.

## Block 3

Eyebrow: Moving things

Headline: A scheduled time is a suggestion that can be moved.

The schedule view lets a person move an Action Item to a new time. Scheduled times are recommendations and assistance, not due dates, because completion is decided at the end of the week. Mondai does not reschedule on its own, since that could get confusing fast.

## Block 4

Eyebrow: Edge cases

Headline: A packed calendar falls back to the plain list.

When the calendar is too full, a notice says it is packed this week and the scheduled times are removed. The idea was to switch that week to the non calendar approach, since a manual flow could be a lot of work for a fringe case. When a sync fails, a module over the list says the calendar lacks available times or could not sync.
