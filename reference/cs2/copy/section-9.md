Section: 9

## Block 1

Eyebrow: Fresh start

Headline: A clean slate opens a fresh week for someone ahead or on target.

There are three versions: a fresh week ahead or on target, a new Pathway beginning, and ahead with positive momentum. A clean slate never includes a lost day, because a lost day means a rollover.

## Block 2

Eyebrow: Behind a bit

Headline: Rollover shows where someone is sitting and what moves with them.

It names the days lost and the Action Items moving into the new week. It has versions for someone still ahead and for someone who slipped from ahead to on track. The header says why the person is looking at the screen.

## Block 3

Eyebrow: Recovery

Headline: Catching up has to be earned by real movement.

Catching up shows a gap with no gain and no loss, or modest gains over consecutive recovery weeks while still behind. Closing exactly everything is not catching up. Caught up resolves it, after one week behind or after two or more.

## Block 4

Eyebrow: Capacity

Headline: Recalibration answers weeks of falling short.

After weeks of falling short, a recommendation offers a new capacity. The person can set a custom one, and there is a skip warning.

## Block 5

Eyebrow: Long absence

Headline: Reset rebuilds the week from today.

After a long absence, progress is saved and the week is rebuilt from today. Reset is a start of week variation, not its own flow. The choice was removed, and so was the option to keep the original schedule.
