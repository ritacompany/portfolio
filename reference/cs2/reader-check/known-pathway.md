# What the reader already knows before the Pathway page block

From his portfolio frame 4269:9433 (Portfolio Design file), read 3 Nov with use_figma, text sorted by y,

K1. Mondai is an AI career tool that builds a personalized route into a tech career and fits it into a busy life. When the designer started, the curriculum had a fixed linear order and calendar integration was planned, but no framework connected them.
K2. Like CareerFoundry, Mondai shows two dates side by side: the target the plan sets and the pace date progress is heading for.
K3. A connected calendar suggests where the work fits, and the plan still runs without one.
K4. Like Linear, the home screen opens on the work in front of you and shows only the next steps, because near goals build confidence while a distant goal does little. The full Growth Journey, every step to the goal, has its own page.
K5. The plan keeps a target and a pace while each person fits the work into their own day.
K6. The route to the new career and its curriculum is called the Growth Journey. It is made of five Pathways in order: Discovery, Cognition, Networking, Branding, Opportunity.
K7. Each Pathway has two layers below it: Focus Areas, and Action Items.
K8. The Growth Journey page (image only, copy not yet written) shows one person's journey with a journey target, a journey pace and percent complete, and the five Pathways, each with its own target and pace dates.
