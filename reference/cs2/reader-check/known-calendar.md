# What the reader already knows before the calendar screen block

Fixed list for the reader check. Change it only when an earlier block's approved copy changes.

K1. Each week has one deadline, Saturday night.
K2. Each week is sized to the hours a person says they can give each week (capacity).
K3. The Action Hub is the screen people open most. It lists this week's Action Items, each a piece of work shown as a card (the Action Hub list).
K4. A Pathway target and a Pathway pace show how the current Pathway is going.
K5. Scheduled times (approved copy): A person can move any Action Item to a new time whenever they want, and Mondai never moves one on its own, because times that shift by themselves get confusing fast. The time it starts with is a suggestion, placed around the person's day. If it passes, it drops off the card and the item stays open for the rest of the week.
K6. Calendar setup (approved copy): Calendar sync is optional. People who turn it on answer two quick questions about their day, and Mondai plans around the answers. The first is the hours a person prefers to work. Mondai suggests a range from their synced calendar, and the person adjusts it. The second is when they focus best within those hours. Mondai uses both to decide where Action Items go first. They are preferences, not limits. The range needs at least eight hours, because Mondai can only use the time not already taken by the person's own events. A short range on a busy day leaves none.
K7. A reschedule icon on each card opens a way to pick a new time.

Not yet shown: the calendar view itself.
