# What the reader already knows before the schedule view block, in the proposed order

Fixed list for the reader check. In this order the scheduled times part has moved out of the Action Hub block into the schedule view block, so it is not known yet.

K1. Each week has one deadline, Saturday night.
K2. Each week is sized to the hours a person says they can give each week (capacity).
K3. The Action Hub is the screen people open most. It lists this week's Action Items, each a piece of work shown as a card (the Action Hub list).
K4. A Pathway target and a Pathway pace show how the current Pathway is going.
K6. Calendar setup (working copy, 29 Oct): Syncing a calendar lets this week's Action Items sit between a person's own events, after two quick questions about their day. People's weeks look nothing alike, so setup asks when a person prefers to work (a range Mondai suggests from their calendar, at least eight hours) and when they focus best. Both are preferences, not limits, and decide which gaps Action Items fill first.

K7. A person can move an Action Item to a new time from its card (covered in the Action Hub block, per Chadwick 27 Oct).

Promises earlier blocks set up for this block to pay off (from his frame text, 28 Oct export):

P1. Background (frame y 1510): the MVP had plans for calendar integration but no framework to connect it.
P2. Competitive analysis, Motion card (frame y 2863): AI can find free time in a calendar, but pushing people to schedule did not help them stick with it, so a connected calendar suggests where the work fits and the plan still runs without one.

Not yet shown: the schedule view itself, suggested times.
