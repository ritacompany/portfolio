# What the reader already knows before the Falling behind block

From his frame copy above the block (Desktop 1 MVP file, CS page, 2 Nov).

K1. The Growth Journey is five Pathways, each with Focus Areas and Action Items.
K2. Each week's Action Items are set by capacity, the hours a person says they can give each week, and by cognitive load, how much effort each Action Item takes.
K3. The Action Hub lists this week's Action Items and is the screen people open most.
K4. The Pathway target is the date the Pathway should be done by, set from the person's hours. It holds steady through an ordinary week and moves when those hours change.
K5. The Pathway pace is where progress says they will finish. It updates once a week. The gap between target and pace, in days, is the only ahead or behind signal. There is no streak.
K6. Calendar sync is optional; people who turn it on answer two questions about their day.
K7. The schedule view shows the same week as a calendar, with suggested times; a person can move any time, Mondai never moves one, and a passed time drops off while the item stays open.

Not yet shown: what happens when a week is not finished, the buffer flow, start of week, changing capacity.
