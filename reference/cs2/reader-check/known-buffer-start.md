# What the reader already knows before buffer flow and start of week

From his portfolio frame 4269:9433 (Portfolio Design file), read 3 Nov, in page order. Capacity, cognitive
load and the map now come before Falling behind.

K1. The Growth Journey is five Pathways, each with Focus Areas and Action Items.
K2. The Action Hub lists this week's Action Items and is the screen people open most.
K3. The Pathway target is the date the Pathway should be done by, set from the person's hours. It holds steady through an ordinary week and moves when those hours change.
K4. The Pathway pace is where progress says they will finish. It updates once a week. The gap between target and pace, in days, is the only ahead or behind signal. There is no streak.
K5. The Growth Journey page holds the whole journey, with its own target and pace (placeholder block).
K6. Calendar sync is optional; people who turn it on answer two questions about their day.
K7. The schedule view shows the same week as a calendar, with suggested times; a person can move any time, Mondai never moves one, and a passed time drops off while the item stays open.
K8. Each week's Action Items are set by capacity, the hours a person says they can give each week, and by cognitive load, how much effort each Action Item takes. Example: six hours a week can mean three Action Items one week and four the next.

Known only to a reader of the Falling behind block (the map comes first):
K9. The map block: how capacity, the week, the targets and the paces connect.

Known from Falling behind (3 Nov, the bridge block before these two):
K10. Unfinished Action Items move into the next week on their own, and the new week takes fewer new ones so it still fits the hours given.

Known only when reading V2, because the buffer flow block (V1) comes right before start of week:
K11. When every Action Item of the week is done early, a completion screen celebrates it and offers next week's Action Items to pull forward; its words change with where the person stands against the target.
