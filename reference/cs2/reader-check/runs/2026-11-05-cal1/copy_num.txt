VERSION V1: Schedule view, merged block round 1
  (label) Schedule view
V1.S1 With a calendar synced, the Action Hub can show the same week as a schedule, with Mondai finding a time for the Action Items that fit around the person's day.
V1.S2 The list stays the home view.
V1.S3 Syncing a calendar is optional, so the plan has to work without one, and anyone can switch between the list and the schedule whenever they like.
V1.S4 Mondai looks for gaps between the person's own events, inside the hours they chose in setup.
V1.S5 The top row of the calendar is where those hours start, 8am here.
V1.S6 Each block is as long as its Action Item takes, and this is the only place someone can see how big a piece of work is before they start it.
V1.S7 Mondai only reads the calendar.
V1.S8 It never writes Action Items into it, because that calendar holds commitments Mondai doesn't own.
V1.S9 Action Items and the person's own events each get their own color.
V1.S10 Later I plan to carry over the colors from their calendar settings.
V1.S11 I wanted people free to move their Action Items.
V1.S12 Rescheduling works from the list and from the schedule, and Mondai offers only open time in that week, so nothing lands on a meeting.
V1.S13 Mondai never moves a time on its own.
V1.S14 Automatic moving was easy to build, and I left it out because a plan that rearranges itself gets confusing.
V1.S15 A time only helps someone find room.
V1.S16 The week is what counts, so an Action Item with no time is fine, and finishing it counts the same.
V1.S17 When a week has no room left, the item stays in the list without a time.
