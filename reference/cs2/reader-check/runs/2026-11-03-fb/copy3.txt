=== Falling behind, what was done and why
[eyebrow or sub-heading] Falling behind
[headline] Every Action Item in a week is due by Saturday night. Some weeks, one of them is still open when that night comes, and it has to go somewhere.
A week that comes up short can feel like failing, and that feeling makes people less likely to keep going.
[eyebrow or sub-heading] Rollover
Anything still open on Saturday night rolls over into the next week.
[eyebrow or sub-heading] On its own
It happens without asking, so nobody has to decide what to keep at the moment they're behind.
[eyebrow or sub-heading] Still fits
The next week takes fewer new Action Items to make room and stays the size of the hours someone gave.
