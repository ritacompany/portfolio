VERSION V1: Falling behind, what was done and why
  (label) Falling behind
V1.S1 Every Action Item in a week is due by Saturday night.
V1.S2 Some weeks, one of them is still open when that night comes, and it has to go somewhere.
V1.S3 A week that comes up short can feel like failing, and that feeling makes people less likely to keep going.
  (label) Rollover
V1.S4 Anything still open on Saturday night rolls over into the next week.
  (label) On its own
V1.S5 It happens without asking, so nobody has to decide what to keep at the moment they're behind.
  (label) Still fits
V1.S6 The next week takes fewer new Action Items to make room and stays the size of the hours someone gave.
