VERSION V1: Falling behind with the four ways diagram
  (label) Falling behind
V1.S1 Every Action Item in a week is due by Saturday night.
V1.S2 Some weeks, one of them is still open when that night comes, and it has to go somewhere.
  (label) Ruled out: Push forward
V1.S3 Everything left lands in next week, which gets heavier right after a hard one.
  (label) Ruled out: Let them pick
V1.S4 They decide what to keep at the moment they are behind.
  (label) Ruled out: Drop it
V1.S5 The leftover disappears and the Pathway slips without anyone seeing it.
  (label) Kept: Carry over
V1.S6 The week stays the size of their capacity.
V1.S7 A week that comes up short can feel like failing, and that feeling makes people less likely to keep going.
V1.S8 So anything left over moves into the next week on its own, and that week takes fewer new Action Items to stay within their capacity.
