VERSION V1: Falling behind
  (label) Falling behind
V1.S1 Every Action Item in a week is due by Saturday night.
V1.S2 Some weeks, one of them is still open when that night comes, and it has to go somewhere.
V1.S3 A week that comes up short can feel like failing, and that feeling makes people less likely to keep going.
V1.S4 So anything left over moves into the next week on its own, and that week takes fewer new Action Items to stay within their capacity.
