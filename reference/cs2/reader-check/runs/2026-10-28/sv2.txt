=== Schedule view, version 1
[eyebrow or sub-heading] The schedule view
[headline] People follow through more often on a plan that names a time, so with calendar sync on, the Action Hub can become a calendar that gives every Action Item one.
Each Action Item gets a suggested time in an open gap between the person's own events, leaning toward the hours they focus best.
In a large field study, asking people to write down a date barely changed whether they followed through. Asking for a date and a time did.
The time is there to help, not to judge. Saturday night is still the only deadline, so a suggested time can pass without anything going wrong.
Source: Milkman, Beshears, Choi, Laibson and Madrian 2011

=== Schedule view, version 2
[eyebrow or sub-heading] The schedule view
[headline] In a list every Action Item looks the same size. On a calendar each one takes up the time it needs, so a person can see how heavy their week is before they start.
Every Action Item carries an estimate of how long it takes. The list has no way to show it, and the length of a block on the calendar is the first place it appears.
A two hour block beside a thirty minute one says more about the week than any number on a card.
People without a synced calendar never see that block, so for them the estimate is still invisible. That gap is still open.

=== Schedule view, version 3
[eyebrow or sub-heading] The schedule view
[headline] Once Action Items had times on a calendar, three features could have turned a helpful suggestion into a schedule to fail, and I said no to all three.
[eyebrow or sub-heading] No moving on its own
Times change only when the person changes them. A plan that rearranges itself is hard to trust, and people keep using a tool more often when they keep the final say.
[eyebrow or sub-heading] No deadline per item
People underestimate how long their own work takes, so a due time on every Action Item would be missed constantly, while one deadline for the whole week leaves room to misjudge.
[eyebrow or sub-heading] No missed state
Mondai cannot see whether the work happened at the suggested time. When the time passes, the Action Item simply stays open for the week.
Sources: Dietvorst, Simmons and Massey 2018; Buehler, Griffin and Ross 1994
