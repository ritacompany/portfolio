=== Start of week, as in the frame now
[eyebrow or sub-heading] Start of week
[headline] Whether someone finished last week early or fell short, the next week starts with a recap and a plan for the week.
This is the first screen someone sees when they open the product each week. It lays out the week ahead, and what's on it depends on what they need that week. If they're on track, it says less. If they're struggling to stay on target, it offers ways to catch up.
[caption, template Clean slate] A clean slate only means something if no day was lost, so a week with a lost day goes to rollover.
[caption, template Catching up] Finishing every Action Item doesn't close the gap for someone behind, so this screen shows the days they won back and points to finishing early, until they catch up.
[caption, template Rollover] When a week ends unfinished, the rollover Action Items wait in the new week under a magenta tag. The screen shows where the pace landed and what carried over, so the plan never changes unseen.
[caption, template Recalibration] Rollover covers one short week. When short weeks keep coming, the screen suggests a capacity that fits the hours someone has been giving. They can take it, set their own or skip it with a warning.
[caption, template Reset] After a long absence, the old plan is too far behind to catch up to, so the week rebuilds from today with progress kept and new target dates. It has one button, because a keep my plan option would win by default.
