VERSION V1: Map, version C
  (label) How it connects
V1.S1 The small number beside a target, two days ahead or three behind, is the end of a chain that starts with one answer about a person's week.
V1.S2 Working back from that number, it is the distance between what a person said and what they did, since the target comes from the hours they promised and the pace from the Action Items they finished.
V1.S3 The same hours size each week, with Action Items added in order until their effort fills the time, so a week never weighs more than the person said they had.
V1.S4 That is why hours are the only thing anyone sets.
V1.S5 I picked them over a fixed weekly load, which would suit neither someone fitting this around a job nor someone giving it forty hours.
V1.S6 The same number sets six dates, a target for each of the five Pathways and one for the journey.
V1.S7 Hours can change from the journey's target, at the start of a week or in settings.
V1.S8 The control sits with the journey because a new number moves every date.
V1.S9 It starts next week unless the person picks now, and dates update on save, not while typing, so the screen keeps the plan's weekly rhythm.
