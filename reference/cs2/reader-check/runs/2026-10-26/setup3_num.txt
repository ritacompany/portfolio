VERSION V1: Calendar setup
  (label) Calendar setup
V1.S1 Calendar sync unlocks the schedule view, where this week's Action Items sit around the person's own calendar.
V1.S2 Turning it on takes two quick questions about their day.
V1.S3 The first is the hours a person prefers to work.
V1.S4 Mondai suggests a range from their synced calendar, and the person adjusts it.
V1.S5 The second is when they focus best within those hours.
V1.S6 Mondai uses both to decide where Action Items go first.
V1.S7 They are preferences, not limits.
V1.S8 The range needs at least eight hours, because Mondai can only use the time not already taken by the person's own events.
V1.S9 A short range on a busy day leaves none.
