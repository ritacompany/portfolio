# What the reader already knows, per version

Built 7 Nov from reference/cs2/review-2026-11-07/final-copy.md, the page in order (Figma frame 5763:2448 with his
review decisions applied). The five versions sit at different points on the page, so each fact says which
versions may count it as known. When judging a version, use only the facts marked for it. A fact not marked for
a version is unknown to that version's reader.

Known before V1 (the hero, top of the page): nothing.

Known before V2, V3, V4 and V5:
K1. Mondai builds each user a curriculum for the tech career they choose, called their Growth Journey; it can take months. Most users don't have the same hours free every week.
K2. Mondai shows two dates side by side: the target date the plan sets and the pace date the user's progress points to.
K3. A connected calendar suggests where work fits; the plan runs without one.
K4. The home screen shows only this week's work, and the full route to the goal has its own page.
K5. The Growth Journey is five Pathways, taken one at a time in order. Each Pathway holds Action Items, the individual pieces of work, grouped into Focus Areas by theme.
K6. The Growth Journey page shows the whole journey, with the journey's own target, pace and progress.
K7. Each Pathway has its own page, with its Action Items in order, including locked ones, its target and pace and its Focus Areas.

Known before V3, V4 and V5 only:
K8. The Action Hub is the screen of this week's Action Items; each week opens with a headline and paragraph the AI writes; Action Items are finished in order and only the Active one can be checked off.
K9. Every Action Item is a piece of work the AI writes, on a page of plain text containers.
K10. Capacity is the hours a user says they can give each week, set the first time they open the Action Hub; everything is planned from it. Effort is how much work each Action Item takes. Example: Aisha's capacity is 6 hours a week; last week that fit 4 Action Items, this week 3.
K11. Calendar sync is optional; users who turn it on set a time range and their sharpest hours.
K12. With a calendar synced, the Action Hub can show the week as a schedule. Times are suggestions; an Action Item is due by the end of its week, Saturday night. Mondai never moves a time on its own.

Known before V4 and V5 only:
K13. The Action Hub's right rail shows the Pathway target (set from weekly hours, holds steady through an ordinary week, moves when those hours change), the Pathway pace (where progress says they will finish, updates once a week) and the gap between them in days, the only ahead or behind signal.

Known before V5 only:
K14. Rollover: an unfinished Action Item moves into the next week on its own, takes up some of that week's capacity, and pushes the pace later; the target stays.
K15. Buffer flow: finishing a week early offers next week's Action Items to pull forward, the only way to move the pace ahead.
K16. Start of week: the first screen each Sunday, in five versions (Clean slate, Catching up, Rollover, Recalibration, Reset). Recalibration suggests a capacity that fits the hours the user has been giving when Rollover keeps happening.
