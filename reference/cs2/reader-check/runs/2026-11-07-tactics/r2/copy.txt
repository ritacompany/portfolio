=== Growth Journey block, with t-gj
[headline] The whole route has a page of its own, one users open now and then to see how far they’ve come and what’s left.
Users need to see how the Pathway they’re on fits into the whole journey, so this page zooms out to show all of it.
Long term metrics like their journey target, pace and progress sit here out of the user’s immediate view.
Keeping them here was intentional. Two sets of dates on the weekly screen would compete, so the journey’s sit here for anyone who wants the big picture.
=== Action Hub block, with t-hub and t-hub-active
[eyebrow or sub-heading] Action Hub
[headline] Most of a user’s week happens on one screen: the list of Action Items they come back to nearly every day.
I designed the Action Hub around one question for the user: what do I need to do this week?
Each week opens with a short headline and a paragraph the AI writes about that week’s work. It should read like a friend telling the user what the week is about before they look at their list, so it talks about the work itself and not about how much they’ve finished.
Each Action Item builds on the one before, so they’re finished in order and only the one at the top, marked Active, can be checked off.
=== Right rail block, with t-rail
[headline] The Action Hub keeps two dates for the Pathway the user is on: where they’re aiming and where they’re headed.
The right rail shows both, and the target stays put on purpose. If it moved every time a week went badly, the user would never see that they’d slipped, and their pace falling behind a target that holds is what’s meant to get them back on track.
[eyebrow or sub-heading] Pathway target
The date this Pathway should be done by, set from the hours the user gives each week. It holds steady through an ordinary week and moves when those hours change.
[eyebrow or sub-heading] Pathway pace
Where the user’s progress says they will finish. It updates once a week, so nobody watches it tick with every finished item. In week one it matches the target.
[eyebrow or sub-heading] Ahead of target
The gap between the two dates, in days. It is the only ahead or behind signal on the rail.
=== Rollover block, with t-roll-head and t-roll-body
[headline] When an Action Item is still open on Saturday night, everything after it moves a little later.
[eyebrow or sub-heading] Rollover: An Action Item still open when the week ends on Saturday night.
[eyebrow or sub-heading] The Rollover effect
A week that comes up short can feel like failing, and that feeling makes users less likely to keep going. So nobody has to decide what to do with it while they’re already behind: an unfinished Action Item moves into the next week on its own. Asking users to choose at that moment, with the math on screen, makes it feel like a gate instead of a guide. The Rollover takes up some of the next week’s capacity, so some of that week’s Action Items move to the week after, and the ones behind them move along too. That is why the pace date moves later, and since the journey pace is the last Pathway’s pace, the journey moves with it. The target stays where it was.
