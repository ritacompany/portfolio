# What the reader already knows, per version (final copy, 8 Nov)

Built from final-copy.md after rounds 2 and 3, page in order. Version n is block n. Fact Kn is what block n told the reader, so it is known only to versions after n (K1 to V2 onward, K2 to V3 onward, and so on). When judging a version, use only the facts with a lower number.

K1 (known to V2 onward). Mondai builds each user a curriculum for their chosen tech career, called their Growth Journey, taking months. Users don't have the same hours every week, so a weekly framework turns it into weeks they can finish, planned around the hours they can give. Mondai hasn't launched; the case study shows reasoning.
K2 (known to V3 onward). Mondai shows a target date (set by the plan) and a pace date (where progress points), like CareerFoundry. A connected calendar suggests where work fits; the plan runs without one. The home screen shows only this week's work. The plan sets the goal and the user decides where in their day the work goes.
K3 (known to V4 onward). The Growth Journey is five Pathways, one at a time in order; each Pathway holds Action Items, the pieces of work, grouped into Focus Areas.
K4 (known to V5 onward). The Growth Journey has its own page with the journey's target, pace and progress, kept off the weekly screen.
K5 (known to V6 onward). Each Pathway has its own page with its Action Items in order, locked ones included, its target and pace and Focus Areas.
K6 (known to V7 onward). The Action Hub is the screen of this week's Action Items, opened nearly every day; each week opens with an AI written headline and paragraph; items are finished in order and only the top one, marked Active, can be checked off.
K7 (known to V8 onward). The AI writes each Action Item as text on a page of ten kinds of plain text containers.
K8 (known to V9 onward). Capacity is the hours a user says they can give each week, typed in the first time they open the Action Hub; everything is planned from it, and the plan can lower it later if weeks keep falling short. Effort is how much work each Action Item takes. Aisha is an example user: capacity 6 hours, 4 Action Items last week, 3 this week.
K9 (known to V10 onward). Calendar sync is optional; users who turn it on set a time range and their sharpest hours.
K10 (known to V11 onward). With sync on, the Action Hub can show the week as a schedule; Mondai finds times in gaps; the user can move times; an Action Item is due by the end of its week, Saturday night, and its time is a suggestion.
K11 (known to V12 onward). A rail on the right of the Action Hub shows the Pathway target (set from capacity, holds steady through an ordinary week), the Pathway pace (updates once a week) and the gap in days.
K12 (known to V13 onward). Rollover: an Action Item still open on Saturday night moves into the next week on its own, takes some of that week's capacity and pushes the pace later; the target stays.
K13 (known to V14 onward). Buffer flow: finishing the week early opens a screen that offers next week's Action Items to pull forward, the only way to move the pace ahead.
K14 (known to V15 onward). Start of week: the first screen each Sunday, in five versions: Clean slate, Catching up, Rollover, Recalibration (suggests a capacity that fits the hours actually given), Reset.
