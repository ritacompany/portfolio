=== Rollover
[eyebrow or sub-heading] Rollover
[headline] So what happens to the Action Items someone doesn't get to by Saturday night? They have to go somewhere.
A missed step on a long goal is when people give up, so where leftovers go is a motivation question. Pushing it all forward makes next week heavier than the hours the person gave. Asking them to sort it hands them a pile of decisions while they are behind. Dropping it lets the Pathway slip quietly.
So leftovers move into next week on their own and count first against the same hours. Carried items are marked in soft magenta, not a warning color, so they read as carried forward, because showing a lapse lowers later engagement. Pace updates once a week. A run of hard weeks is a different problem, which the plan handles when it rechecks the hours a person gave.
Sources: Rai, Sharif, Chang, Milkman and Duckworth 2022; Silverman and Barasch 2023
