=== Calendar setup
[eyebrow or sub-heading] Calendar setup
[headline] Calendar sync unlocks the schedule view, where this week's Action Items sit between a person's own events, after two quick questions about their day.
I didn't want the plan to feel like a school timetable, so setup asks two things a calendar can't say. First, when a person prefers to work, since a free hour at 6am isn't time most people would use. Mondai suggests a range from their calendar and they adjust it. It needs at least eight hours, so a busy day still has open time.
Second, when they focus best, because demanding work tends to go better at a person's peak hours. Mondai uses both answers to decide which gaps Action Items fill first. They are preferences, not limits, because the evidence on peak hours is real but modest and plenty of days don't go as planned.
Source: May, Hasher and Healey 2023
