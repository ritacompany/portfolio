VERSION V1: The schedule view
  (label) The schedule view
V1.S1 The same week can also be laid out by time, so a person sees where each Action Item fits in their day and can flip back to the list whenever they like.
V1.S2 Each Action Item gets a time because naming a time works better than naming a day.
V1.S3 In a flu shot study, a date alone barely moved turnout, while a date and a time did.
V1.S4 The time stays a suggestion.
V1.S5 People underestimate their own work, so Saturday night is still the only deadline.
V1.S6 We kept the sync one way.
V1.S7 Mondai reads the calendar to find gaps between events and never writes anything back, because a suggestion written into a person's calendar would look like a commitment they never made.
V1.S8 Sources: Milkman, Beshears, Choi, Laibson and Madrian 2011; Buehler, Griffin and Ross 1994
