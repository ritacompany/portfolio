=== The schedule view
[eyebrow or sub-heading] The schedule view
[headline] People who sync their calendar also get the schedule view, which shows exactly where each of their Action Items fits in the week they already have.
It sits beside the list, and a person can flip between the two whenever they like. Each Action Item gets a time because a specific time is what helps: in a flu shot study, a date alone barely moved turnout, while a date and a time did.
The time stays a suggestion. People underestimate their own work, so Saturday night is still the only deadline.
We kept the sync one way. Mondai reads the calendar to find gaps between events and never writes anything back, because a suggestion written into a person's calendar would look like a commitment they never made.
Sources: Milkman, Beshears, Choi, Laibson and Madrian 2011; Buehler, Griffin and Ross 1994
