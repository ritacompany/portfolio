VERSION V1: Falling behind, version A
  (label) Falling behind
V1.S1 Every plan built on weeks has to decide what happens when one of them doesn't get finished, and that answer shapes every week after it.
V1.S2 Say Sally had four Action Items this week and finished three.
V1.S3 Her Pathway target doesn't move, because it only changes when the hours she gives change.
V1.S4 A target that slid with every bad week would stop meaning anything.
V1.S5 On Sunday her pace moves to show the later finish.
V1.S6 The unfinished Action Item is already in her new week, and the week takes fewer new ones, so it still fits her hours and doesn't punish her for last week.
V1.S7 Three mechanisms keep a short week from turning into a pile up, each at a different moment.
V1.S8 The first is the buffer flow, a screen that opens when someone finishes their week early, so they can catch up or get ahead.
V1.S9 The second is start of week, a recap that opens every new week and shows where they stand.
V1.S10 If weeks keep falling short, the plan looks at their progress and suggests fewer hours, which they can also change themselves in settings.

VERSION V2: Falling behind, version B
  (label) Falling behind
V2.S1 When a week ends with work unfinished, the target, the pace and the next week all react, so each one got a rule for how far it can move.
V2.S2 Take Sally, with four Action Items this week and three finished.
V2.S3 The rule I set is that one short week changes as little as possible.
V2.S4 Her target stays where it was, since only a change in her hours moves it.
V2.S5 Her pace moves once, on Sunday, to show the later finish.
V2.S6 The unfinished Action Item carries into the new week on its own, and the week takes fewer new ones so it still fits her hours.
V2.S7 Three mechanisms handle what one week can't.
V2.S8 The buffer flow, a screen for finishing a week early, covers the good weeks.
V2.S9 The start of week recap, which opens every new week, shows where she stands.
V2.S10 Capacity covers a run of short weeks, when the plan looks at her progress and suggests fewer hours.
V2.S11 She can also change her hours herself in settings.

VERSION V3: Falling behind, version C
  (label) Falling behind
V3.S1 Falling behind one week can't be allowed to grow into a pile of work, so three mechanisms share the job of keeping each week the right size.
V3.S2 The first is the buffer flow, a screen that opens the moment someone finishes their week early and lets them catch up or get ahead.
V3.S3 The second is start of week, a recap that opens every new week and shows where they stand.
V3.S4 Capacity steps in when weeks keep falling short.
V3.S5 The plan looks at their progress and suggests fewer hours, and they can change it themselves in settings.
V3.S6 Sally's week shows them working together.
V3.S7 She had four Action Items and finished three.
V3.S8 Her target stays put, because only her hours move it.
V3.S9 On Sunday her pace shows the later finish, and the unfinished one is already in her new week, which takes fewer new Action Items so it still fits.
