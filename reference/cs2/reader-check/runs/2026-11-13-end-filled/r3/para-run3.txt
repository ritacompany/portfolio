TESTED: Nobody had used the weekly plan for a real week, so eight people used a plan sized to their own typed hours for two weeks and logged sessions, to check three things: that people give hours they can really spend, that the target and pace dates make sense without explanation, and that carried over work reads as help. Real use was chosen over a click-through because a click-through only shows what people say they would do.
NUMBERS: 6 of 8 means six people spent at least four fifths of the hours they typed, so the hours they gave were realistic. 7 of 8 means seven correctly predicted how a short week would change their dates. 6 of 8 means six saw carried over work as the plan helping them.
CLOSE: The pass line was that a test fails when 4 of 8 or fewer succeed, all three cleared it, and the weak spot was the two people who typed too many hours and got overfilled plans, so Recalibration now offers a lower number after two short weeks instead of three.
