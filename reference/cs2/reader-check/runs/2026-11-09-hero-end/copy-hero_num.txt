VERSION V1: A: the user's situation, then the project (Haven's order)
  (label) Role: Founding designer. Affiliate: Mondai. Focus: Design, research and strategy.
V1.S1 Designed as an AI guide for newcomers from underrepresented backgrounds finding their way into tech.
V1.S2 There’s no set path into tech, so Mondai’s AI writes each user a curriculum for the career they choose, and it can take months.
V1.S3 Most users don’t have the same hours free every week, so a plan that expects a steady pace would leave them behind from week one.
V1.S4 When I started on the MVP, the curriculum was a list in a fixed order, with calendar integration planned and nothing connecting them.
V1.S5 I designed the weekly framework that turns it into weeks each user can finish.
V1.S6 Mondai hasn’t launched, so this case study shows the reasoning rather than results.

VERSION V2: B: what existed, the question it left, then the answer
  (label) Role: Founding designer. Affiliate: Mondai. Focus: Design, research and strategy.
V2.S1 Designed as an AI career navigator for the next generation of hustlers from underrepresented backgrounds.
V2.S2 When I started on Mondai’s MVP, the curriculum its AI writes for each user already had a fixed order, and calendar integration was planned.
V2.S3 There was no way yet to decide how much of it belongs in a single week, and the whole curriculum can take months.
V2.S4 The answer depends on the user, because most of them don’t have the same hours free every week.
V2.S5 So I designed a weekly framework that plans each week around the hours they say they can give and still holds when a week runs short.
V2.S6 Mondai hasn’t launched, so what follows is the reasoning behind each decision rather than results.

VERSION V3: C: two users, one curriculum
  (label) Role: Founding designer. Affiliate: Mondai. Focus: Design, research and strategy.
V3.S1 Designed as a way for newcomers from underrepresented backgrounds to find a path into tech that fits around their life.
V3.S2 Two users can choose the same tech career and have very different weeks.
V3.S3 One can give 5 hours a week and the other 15, and either can have a week where almost nothing gets done.
V3.S4 Mondai’s AI writes each of them a curriculum that can take months, so one framework has to work for both.
V3.S5 I designed the weekly framework that turns it into weeks they can finish, planned from the hours they say they can give.
V3.S6 Mondai hasn’t launched, so this case study shows the reasoning: how much lands in a week and what happens when a week runs short.
