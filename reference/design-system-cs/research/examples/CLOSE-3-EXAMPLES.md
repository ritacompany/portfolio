## What to take for CS3 from the three passes

- Section 4 (proving the checks): Singh's June page seeds known mistakes and counts how many the check catches, which is the same move as Chadwick's rule that a check counts only after it catches planted mistakes. Take the habit of reporting the false alarm count next to the catch count.
- Section 5 (the agent test): Kukkasela states that her 18 runs show a direction and not a measurement, and that her own server's error sat inside the 67% result. Take the plain line on what the test cannot show, and the fact that her mistake lived in the system, not the agent.
- Section 6 (still imperfect): Singh's September page lists what the rerun did not fix. Kukkasela ends on what she cannot answer yet. Both are models for a dated list of what is open.
- Section 3 (layout and components): Bailey's schema per component and his habit of filing agent failures as tickets against the system is the closest new match to the model's contract and triage.
