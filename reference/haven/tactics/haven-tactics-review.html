What this pass found

The CS2 tactics pass, run on Haven, 9 Nov. I pulled every eyebrow, headline, body, caption, card and label from the coded Haven page into one copy source (reference/haven/final-copy.md). Then I read both banks entry by entry: the case study tactics bank (49 entries) and the reader effect bank (44). Haven copy went through earlier AI passes, so I assumed no line was yours and no block used a tactic. For each block I asked one thing: would an entry make it do its job better for a hiring manager reading cold?

21 proposals below, in page order. Each names the entry it comes from, or the copy rule of yours it applies (typos, a fragment, people where you write users). The ones that matter most: the hero gets a decision and says what a Confidante is (2), the 35% caption stops reading as fewer attempts when the study measured odds (3), the wireframes headline names the three ideas testing later changed (9) and the quiz headline says which option lost (15). One proposal needs your yes on a fact: why identity comes before community (8). The page never says, so the reason given is my reading of it. My pick there is Discuss.

Two things I did not propose, because they are yours to call. First, the Security features body is in the HTML but current.css hides it at every width, so that block is its headline and two screens. Did you mean to drop it? Second, the ending never says what testing could not show, such as how the setup holds up at home. Who the six testers were is not in the repo, so I left it alone rather than guess.

Dependencies: 8 only makes sense with 7, and 16 only with 15. If you accept 7 and reject 8, the old body repeats the new headline, so its first sentence goes.
