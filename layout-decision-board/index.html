What stays fixed Clear explanatory copy, a visual field with room to breathe, plus a caption or source.

What can vary Column side, diagram scale, image crop and whether a display word becomes a background layer.

Overlap rule An artifact may intersect oversized display type only. It must never cover source notes, body copy or a readable chart axis.
