Introduction

Creating a flagship destination worthy of one of the world’s most iconic brands. We partnered with Calvin Klein to elevate the brand and rebuild their digital experience with a product mindset.

Taking a product-based approach to ecommerce, we began with a multi-point audit across visuals, content, heuristics and technology to find the biggest user experience opportunities. Then, we set our roadmap to optimize for impact.

The vision

Craft a responsive platform that seamlessly couples brand and commerce.

Form and function

The rich, brand-centric destination exudes personality while also enabling effortless shopping.

As part of a larger initiative to elevate and optimize CK’s digital experience, we overhauled each product detail page in design and engineering.

The goal was to introduce more editorial opportunities while amplifying the details and tools that increase conversion.

Product-based enhancements

A stripped back interface allows the storytelling of each item to shine while maintaining ecommerce best practices.

In the details

CK’s new design system, Holistic, fosters efficiency and consistency at scale.

We needed to construct a design system for CK to ensure consistency and performance at scale. The navigation became our first point of interest. Mixing functional and expressive motion provides an innovative wayfinding solution to focus and guide users.

Being in The Fold

Concepting and ideating loyalty through a collection of exclusive members-only rewards.

To complement the new elevated digital experience, our team envisioned what the future of loyalty could look like with CK.

Harkening back to the intimacy of close relationships, we called it ‘The Fold.’

What if loyalty meant offering brand enthusiasts an exclusive and elegant collection of member benefits and brand identity swag?

Small details, massive reach
