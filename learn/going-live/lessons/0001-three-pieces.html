Three pieces of a website, and where yours are

Your winYou can name the three things a site needs and say where each of yours lives today. Everything else in this course hangs off these three words.

The three pieces

Domain

The address

The name people type. You rent it a year at a time from a company called a registrar.

Yours: chadwickfenner.com, rented from Wix
Hosting

The building

A computer that is always on and hands your files to anyone who visits the address.

Yours: not set up yet. Plan is Vercel, free
Code

What is inside

The files that make the pages: HTML for structure, CSS for looks, JavaScript for motion.

Yours: done and growing, in GitHub
You did not lose the domain

I checked the public registry record on 6 Nov 2026. chadwickfenner.com is still registered to you at Wix, first registered Nov 2022, and paid through 26 Nov 2026. Confirmed You do not need to buy it again. You need to keep it, and that has a 20 day clock. Lesson 2 covers it.

How they connect

A fourth thing, DNS, ties the address to the building. It is a short list of settings attached to your domain that says "when someone asks for chadwickfenner.com, send them to Vercel." Setting it up is two lines of text, typed once.

Why this is the easy kind of site

Your site is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript sitting in the site/ folder. No database, no login, no server code. A site like that is a stack of files, and handing out files is the simplest job a host does. It is why hosting can be free and why there is little that can break after launch.

Framer feels simpler because it bundles all three pieces into one monthly subscription. You would pay for that bundle every month, and you would rebuild the site you already have inside Framer to get it.
