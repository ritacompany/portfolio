The rule on the free hosting plan

Vercel's free plan is for personal, non commercial sites. Their examples of commercial use are taking payments, advertising a product or service for sale, being paid to build the site, affiliate links and ads. Confirmed A portfolio that helps you get hired matches none of those. My reading If you ever sell freelance services from the site, the hosting moves to Cloudflare Pages, which is also free. You would not pay for Vercel's paid plan, and you do not need it now. Ignore the upgrade prompts.

Deadline: 26 Nov 2026. Start by 14 Nov

The move takes up to 7 days once it starts, so starting by 14 Nov leaves room for one retry. If the domain lapses, you get about 30 days to renew at the normal price, then a redemption period with an extra fee, then the name is released for anyone to buy. Wix's own description
