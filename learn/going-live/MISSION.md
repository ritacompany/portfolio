# Mission

## Why

Chadwick's portfolio is close to done and built in plain code in this repo, not in Framer. He has
never put a site online this way, money is tight (he is borrowing), and he is not a coder, so he
cannot audit the code himself. He wants to know the real costs, whether this route is realistic,
what the steps are and how to stay safe without reading code.

## Goal

chadwickfenner.com live with the portfolio, for the least money that costs nothing in quality,
with every step understood well enough to approve it.

## Done looks like

- The domain is kept and moved to a cheaper registrar before it expires on 26 Nov 2026.
- He can say what the domain, hosting, DNS and code each are, and where his live.
- He reviews every change on a preview link and approves it there.
- The site launches on Vercel at chadwickfenner.com, indexing on (launch tracker S4).

## Constraints

- Budget: as close to zero as possible. Year one target is about $12.
- He does not read code. Review happens on the rendered site.
- Short lessons. One win each.

Set 6 Nov 2026 from his request in the session that started this workspace. Confirm with him
before changing it.
