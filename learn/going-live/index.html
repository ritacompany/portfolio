The missionPut chadwickfenner.com online for the least money that costs nothing in quality, and understand each step well enough to approve it without reading code.

Do first: move the domain by 14 Nov

chadwickfenner.com is still yours at Wix, paid through 26 Nov 2026. Lesson 2 has the checklist. Everything else can wait; this cannot.

The short answer

  • Realistic? Yes. The site is already built as plain files, the simplest kind there is to host. Framer would cost about ten times as much and mean rebuilding it.
  • Cost? About $12 for year one: the domain. Hosting is free.
  • Safe for a non coder? Yes, because you review the site on a preview link before anything goes live, and every change can be undone. The domain is the only thing that can't.
