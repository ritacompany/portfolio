# Resources

Ranked by trust. Primary sources first. Checked 6 Nov 2026.

## Primary, high trust

- [What is a domain name?](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Learn_web_development/Howto/Web_mechanics/What_is_a_domain_name), MDN. Plain explanation of domains, registrars and DNS. Lesson 1.
- [Transferring your Wix domain away from Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/transferring-your-wix-domain-away-from-wix-2477749), Wix help. The exact steps on his registrar today. Lesson 2.
- [Vercel fair use guidelines](https://vercel.com/docs/limits/fair-use-guidelines). Free plan limits (100 GB a month) and the non commercial rule with its examples.
- [Vercel environments](https://vercel.com/docs/deployments/environments). Preview and production deployments. Lesson 3.
- [Vercel instant rollback](https://vercel.com/docs/instant-rollback). Free plan rolls back to the previous deployment.
- [Cloudflare: transfer a domain to Cloudflare](https://developers.cloudflare.com/registrar/get-started/transfer-domain-to-cloudflare/). Requirements, including Cloudflare DNS before the move and DNSSEC off. Why Porkbun was picked instead.
- [Porkbun .com](https://porkbun.com/tld/com). $11.81 list price, free WHOIS privacy.
- [Public registry record for chadwickfenner.com](https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/chadwickfenner.com). Registrar Wix, expires 26 Nov 2026, DNSSEC on.
