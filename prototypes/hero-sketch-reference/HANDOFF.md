## Flowers (picked 18 Nov, not yet in the drawing)
- Right side: nodding hellebore, "but veiny", so the veins cover each petal as a net. The noodle puff flowers stay on the right side too.
- Coral (changed 18 Nov): no flowers on the branch tips. Flowers grow only out of the coral surface, next to the metal wire spray on the forks.
- Coral flowers must be blooming flowers with several open petals. No round bells, balloons or pouches (he rejected bladder campion and the bell shapes).
- Coral flower, his pick 18 Nov: candy cane sorrel (Oxalis versicolor). Fully open flowers only (his call, 18 Nov): soft white five petal pinwheels, no striped buds or half open swirls. Dotted over the coral, never coating it. The point is contrast: soft round petals against the spiky metal wire spray on the forks. He sees it going well with the veiny hellebore.
- Right side hellebore: the look he wants is Helleborus x hybridus 'Yellow Spotted', nodding, with dark veins and spots down each petal. Approved 18 Nov after seeing more photos of it. Photos: flower pick sheet, https://claude.ai/artifact/3guhq8RTQSVhqSmRF9q3Zd
- Show flower options as real photos, not line drawings: he wants to see the actual flower. Reference photos from Wikimedia Commons are free. AI renders cost money, so quote the dollar cost first.
- His rules for every flower: cute, nothing cliche, no sunflowers or roses, no big circle flowers, nothing centered like an eye, nothing leaning oddly, flowers big enough to hold their own next to the tube.

## Parts styling (started 18 Nov)
- Method: a more detailed sketch (shape and placement), a flat color version of the same drawing (palette), and a prompt (materials). The AI model makes the photo; we only make sketches.
- Each part gets a pick sheet of real photos with a recommendation, like the flower sheet. Order: pipe, then the parts seen through it (planter, coral base, secondary power connector), then the two cord styles, cord heads (grips, plugs on the box, the secondary connector), spring, box and the stub on its side, then coral, wire spray and noodle puffs, then palette and what glows, then floor or floating, then stem directions last.
- The black spiky bursts on the coral are the wire spray (kit part 76).
- Pipe, his pick 18 Nov: the look of kit part 18, the glass elbow, which was his original inspiration. Satin frosted glass with thick clear coupling rings where each bend meets a straight run and thick rounded rims at the open ends; insides show as soft colored shapes. His change: his pipe has color. The tint is chosen with the palette. Pick sheet: https://claude.ai/artifact/PfG16o5vRrJRqin2bUrB6x
- The pipe's parts: three open ends (both leg tops and the bottom left opening), two elbows, one tee where the crossbar meets the right leg, and the straight runs between.
- His spring idea: a narrow cat toy coil.
- Round 2 (planter, coral base, secondary connector), sheet https://claude.ai/artifact/FE1Y9pDxdk7MnP7DuM9jEo. Not picked. On 20 Nov he said the sheet was confusing and mixed together. Rebuild it before asking again: two separate sections (planter, then connector), each opening with a crop of the current drawing ringing where the part sits, then one big photo per option with its name and one plain sentence, the pick under the options. No kit images unless they show that exact thing, no four box grids, no prompt words on the sheet (keep those here). Options and picks stay the same. Planter: net pot, molded cup, chrome cup, drilled rubber stopper (pick, its taper is already the planter shape and its holes explain the wires). Connector: clear lever connector (pick), molded splitter boot, screw terminal block. Photos are Wikimedia Commons and Openverse; the scratchpad copies were lost, so download them again.
- Planter, his call 24 Nov: "a little pot like thing like simple and colored." A plain smooth little pot in a color from the palette, opaque. No slots, no lab parts, nothing black. He rejected the net pot (a black thing in the middle is ugly) and couldn't picture the rubber stopper. Color comes with the palette.
- Secondary power connector, his yes 24 Nov: a small clear lever connector, a lever for each wire, the thin wires from the pot going in and one cable out to the box. Levers take a palette color.
