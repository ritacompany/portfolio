Keep the bright five.

Your near black and off white already provide the light and dark contrast. Keeping the brighter shade of each hue preserves the lively character of the image while reducing the accent palette to five colors.

On near black

All five accents have enough visual separation for graphic use. The dark field gives the pale yellow and periwinkle their energy.

Use one hero accent at a time

Let near black and off white carry most of the page. Give each section one dominant hue, with a second only when the composition needs a deliberate collision. Showing all five at equal weight should be a rare moment.

Keep text near black on color

The selected accents are strong graphic surfaces, not a universal text set. The dark variants can return later as functional text colors if a real use case requires them, but that would be a separate decision.
