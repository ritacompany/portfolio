Header style: today's spread against narrow buttons

Two structures, not one restyled. Each shows the hero top bar and the nav that appears at the superpowers section (H8). Hover the buttons.

  • Take 1, today's style. Labels spread across the full width in brackets; the nav is three plain words and a white Contact button. It is quiet so the hero's collage carries the page.
  • Take 2, narrow buttons. Everything gathers into one compact cluster at top right on a small dark plate, logo alone at left on a white tab. Each button is narrow and tall with its own small background and does one thing on hover: the words roll to a second line, Contact fills magenta from the bottom (the hero's magenta block). The hero labels become buttons too, so the top bar and the nav are the same object.
  • My recommendation: take 2. The hero is a maximal collage, and take 1's thin labels get lost in it at 1440 (they sit at 12px across 1100px of busy canvas). A tight cluster reads as one deliberate object, gives the hero a second place where your personality shows, and becomes the nav with no change in shape, which makes H8's appear on scroll feel like the same thing arriving, not a new bar. What would change it: if the final hero elements crowd the top right corner, take 1 stays out of their way.
  • Open: I could not find the VS Goods screenshot in HERO INSPIRATION or the Notes & assets boards, so take 2 is built from your description (narrow, creative, small background). If you point me at the frame, I will tune it to the reference.
