Buttons

Two free rebuilds of the paid Framer buttons

Left is the five dollar one: label and icon sit merged at rest and pull apart on hover. Right is the ten dollar one: the outline bends around the pointer and settles back. The blue fill from the original is left out on purpose. Both are plain HTML, CSS and a little JavaScript, no library.

Gooey split, CSS and one SVG filter

The blur plus a hard alpha threshold is what fuses the two shapes. Hover moves the circle 24px and the filter does the rest.

Liquid outline, SVG path on springs

Sixty points on the pill's edge, each on a spring to its rest spot and tied to its neighbours. The pointer pushes nearby points; damping brings them home.
