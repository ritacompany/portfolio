Recommendation

Take 02 forward.

Soft square improves the large form through one rule applied everywhere: flatter curves, straighter runs and consistent bevels. It retains enough of the original at 48 pixels. The choice is whether that firmer character feels like Chadwick's mark; if the original's handwritten softness is essential, keep the source.
