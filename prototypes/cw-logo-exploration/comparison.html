One gesture, drawn with more intent.

This pass compares the original and the previous Soft square pick with three new systems. The test is whether the C, W and terminals feel designed together when the mark grows beyond the header.

Recommendation

Take Structured curve forward.

It changes the shape of the C and both W valleys, not just their endpoints. That is the visible gain over Soft square at 490 pixels. Its weight still survives at 48 pixels. The next decision is visual taste: whether this firmer, more typographic character feels like Chadwick's signature.
