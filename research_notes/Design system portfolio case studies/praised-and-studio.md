## 2. What the strongest share in structure and visual storytelling, and how they balance system and product

### Takeaway
The strongest (Linear, 1Password, Connis, and among studios ustwo and Metalab Ro) open on a concrete situation or a reversal, move through named decisions in the order they were made, and show the system acting inside real screens or a pipeline, with one before and after that carries a number. They keep the product on screen and let the system be the reason the product changed.

### Cited Findings
- Opening on a tension or reversal, not a definition: Connis "I made AI prototypes more consistent. Stakeholders assumed they were final." Source: [Connis](https://daveconnis.com/case-study-3) (hero); 1Password "At first, it was a semi-hot mess." Source: [1Password](https://1password.com/blog/agent-driven-design-system) (intro); Fantasy "BNY had intelligence scattered across systems. Eliza brought it into one." Source: [Fantasy](https://fantasy.co/work/bny-eliza) (Overview)
- Headings as questions or decisions in time order: Linear uses "What tests did we run before getting into implementation?", "What milestones did we use?", "How did we prioritize the refresh work with other projects?" Source: [Linear](https://linear.app/now/how-we-redesigned-the-linear-ui); Connis pairs each heading with its finding, "Designing expectations | When more detail made output worse" Source: [Connis](https://daveconnis.com/case-study-3)
- One before and after with a baseline number: Linear "instead of having to define 98 specific variables for each theme, we defined three" and a captioned "Before / Concept / After" triptych Source: [Linear](https://linear.app/now/how-we-redesigned-the-linear-ui) (Appearance; From a concept to prototype); Connis "from 2.8 to 3.6" Source: [Connis](https://daveconnis.com/case-study-3)
- Multiple named voices: each Linear section carries its author, e.g. "Andreas Eldh, Engineering" explains the LCH colour model Source: [Linear](https://linear.app/now/how-we-redesigned-the-linear-ui) (Appearance)
- A "what we'd do differently" close: 1Password "Ticket quality is not automatable." Source: [1Password](https://1password.com/blog/agent-driven-design-system) (What we'd do differently); Connis "The pattern across each work" Source: [Connis](https://daveconnis.com/case-study-3)
- Studio visual pattern, motion and in context: Calvin Klein shows its system through a "Motion System Animation" module tied to navigation; Ro adds a code module and a stat row counting system parts Source: Metalab content API (Calvin Klein "In the details"; Ro "A design system built for speed and scale", "[Ro] Stat Rows")
- Studio balance is product first: Ro gives the system one of about eleven beats; frog gives it one clause ("a reimagined MyBupa app featuring a new design system and improved governance") Source: Metalab content API (Ro); [frog Bupa](https://www.frog.co/work/designing-the-future-of-health-insurance) (Fresh features and offerings)
- Where the system is the product goal, it is stated as an objective: Fantasy objective 04 "Deliver a design system BNY's team can extend and scale across the platform on its own" Source: [Fantasy](https://fantasy.co/work/bny-eliza) (Our Objectives)

### Inferences
- Judgment: the balance that works is "product as the stage, system as the cause". Linear never separates the system from the app's own views, yet the reader leaves knowing the token model. Instrument Nike shows only product and says nothing about the system, which is the failure mode of the same balance.
- Judgment: a stat row that counts system parts (Ro's 654+ components) looks like evidence but is output, not outcome. The strongest numbers in the sample are a reduction (98 to 3) and a before and after score (2.8 to 3.6).
- Judgment: motion is the studio device most worth borrowing. Calvin Klein makes the system visible as behaviour in navigation, which a static component sheet cannot do.

### Gaps
- Visual storytelling was judged without viewing images or video; Metalab's motion and Fantasy's eight videos could not be assessed for what they show.

## 3. How a case study can do the normal design system job without reading as documentation

### Takeaway
The ones that cover foundations, components, governance and adoption without reading as docs turn each of those into a decision with a person, a reason and a consequence (Linear on tokens, Auto Trader on naming and governance, ustwo on users of the system, 1Password on contribution workflow). The ones that read as docs use the foundations list as headings (Clay Corsair) or describe parts without choices (Instrument Nike).

### Cited Findings
- Foundations as a decision: Linear's colour story is the switch from HSL to LCH and from 98 variables to three inputs, with accessibility as a consequence: "This allows us to automatically include super high-contrast themes for users who need it for accessibility reasons." Source: [Linear](https://linear.app/now/how-we-redesigned-the-linear-ui) (Appearance)
- Components as a naming problem: Auto Trader's "Promo Section" drifted into "a more generic content block", and the team asks whether to "split this into multiple components" Source: [Dan Donald, Smashing](https://www.smashingmagazine.com/2022/04/artifacts-design-systems/) (What's In A Name?; Understanding Change And Evolution)
- Governance as a workflow with a stance: "One early principle we had was that live code trumps the design. That's a controversial statement in some circles" and a proposal structure (Purpose/Intent, Use Cases, Anatomy, Related Components) Source: [Dan Donald, Smashing](https://www.smashingmagazine.com/2022/04/artifacts-design-systems/) (The Source Of Truth And Making Proposals)
- Adoption as users of the system: "Design systems are products; they have users." and "A design system will live or die depending on how it's adopted within the organisation." with five user types named (designers, developers, publishers, content marketers, external agencies) Source: [ustwo iShares](https://ustwo.com/work/ishares/) (Our approach; Designing for multiple end users)
- Governance and contribution in 2026 terms: skills "covering the core design system contributor workflows" (scaffolding a component, defining tokens, Storybook stories, opening a merge request) and a human gate, "A developer reviews the ticket, decides it's well-scoped, applies a label, and the pipeline fires." Source: [1Password](https://1password.com/blog/agent-driven-design-system) (The fix; step 4)
- Documentation trap example: Clay Corsair's headings run "Component-Based Design System; Adaptable & Consistent; Reimagined Iconography; Scalable Grid System; Motion Design Guidelines" Source: [Clay Corsair](https://clay.global/work/corsair)
- Adoption claimed without measure: Fantasy "the clearest proof is adoption" with no figure Source: [Fantasy](https://fantasy.co/work/bny-eliza) (The Impact); Instrument "the Nike design system ultimately empowers creators across the brand" Source: [Instrument Nike](https://www.instrument.com/work/nike-digital-design-system) (The Advantage)

### Inferences
- Judgment: the test for each foundations or governance beat is whether it names something given up. Linear gave up navigation; Auto Trader gave up design file primacy ("live code trumps the design"); 1Password gave up a single broad skill. Beats without a cost read as documentation.
- Judgment: adoption is the weakest beat everywhere. ustwo's footnoted metrics are the best attempt and still contain an inconsistent figure (4h to 2h stated next to "75%"). A case study that shows one honest adoption measure would stand out, which matches R8's conclusion.
- Judgment: for a design system plus AI story, the agent is a new user of the system, so the "users of the system" beat (ustwo) and the "contributor workflow" beat (1Password) can be told together: who reads the system, human and agent, and what changed for each.

### Gaps
- No page in the sample showed an adoption figure with a baseline (for example share of screens built from system components).

## 4. What is forgettable

### Takeaway
Forgettable pages share four habits: a definition or client size as the opening, foundations or "benefits" as headings, adjectives in place of decisions ("flexible", "scalable", "premium"), and numbers that describe the client or the inventory rather than a change.
