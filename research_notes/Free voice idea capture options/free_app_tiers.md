# Free tiers of iPhone voice note and idea capture apps (October 2026)

Scope: US iPhone user, zero budget, 3 to 10 short memos a day, wants transcription plus automatic organizing, ideally reachable by Claude later. Every limit below carries its source. "UNVERIFIED" means only third party or conflicting sources were found, or the official page did not state it.

## What does each app's free tier allow?

### Takeaway
Only a handful of apps have a real, permanent free tier with AI cleanup: Voicenotes (100 min/week, 30 day visible history), Cleft Basic (5 min recordings, on-device transcription, structured AI output, share sheet export), Tana Free (500 AI credits/month), AudioPen free (3 min per note, small note cap) and Otter Basic (300 min/month, meeting oriented). TalkNotes, Whisper Memos, Reflect and Mem's AI-heavy plans are trial or paid. The Claude connector (Voicenotes MCP) is Pro only.

### Cited Findings

**Voicenotes**
- Basic is free: "100 minutes of transcripts and summaries" per week, shared across memos, meetings and dictation, refreshing weekly. Recordings over the limit are saved ("Unlimited raw recordings") and can be transcribed after the reset: [Voicenotes help: Pricing and billing](https://help.voicenotes.com/en/articles/15389129-pricing-and-billing)
- Free history: "access all your notes and meetings from the past 30 days." Older items are stored but hidden until upgrade: [Voicenotes help](https://help.voicenotes.com/en/articles/15389129-pricing-and-billing)
- Dictation is Pro; free users get "a one-time allocation of free dictation minutes": [Voicenotes help](https://help.voicenotes.com/en/articles/15389129-pricing-and-billing)
- Pro is $9/user/month with unlimited minutes and history. Pro includes the Voicenotes MCP ("Connect your notes to Claude, ChatGPT and your favourite AI tools"); Claude is listed as a Pro integration: [voicenotes.com/pricing](https://voicenotes.com/pricing)
- A watchOS app is listed under downloads; plan differences for Watch, widgets and Action Button are not stated: [voicenotes.com/pricing](https://voicenotes.com/pricing)
- Free tier export: not stated on either official page. UNVERIFIED.

**Cleft Notes**
- Cleft Basic is free: 5 minute recordings, on-device transcription, structured notes from rambles with three output styles (Structured, Structured Prose, Clean Transcript), auto-copy, Local Sync, tags, search, export via share sheet "to any app," widgets, Siri and Shortcuts: [cleftnotes.com/pricing](https://cleftnotes.com/pricing)
- Plus ($6.99/month or $39.99/year) adds 30 minute recordings, custom AI instructions, custom Local Sync targets (Obsidian, Bear), Notion and Zapier integrations: [cleftnotes.com/pricing](https://cleftnotes.com/pricing)
- No cap on number of notes is stated for Basic: [cleftnotes.com/pricing](https://cleftnotes.com/pricing). Whether Basic includes Apple Watch, Action Button and Lock Screen could not be confirmed from the page's feature table (Action Button documented for iPhone 15 Pro and later). UNVERIFIED per tier.

**AudioPen**
- Free: "Up to three minutes per note on the free tier"; all writing styles included, "including the ones you build yourself." Prime: 15 minutes per note; file uploads and restyling/combining notes are Prime only. Prime "starting at $33 for three months": [audiopen.ai/voice-to-text-app](https://audiopen.ai/voice-to-text-app)
- Note storage cap of about 10 notes on free is reported by third parties, not the official page: [TechLearning](https://www.techlearning.com/how-to/audiopen-how-to-use-it-to-teach); [Squared Tech](https://www.squaredtech.co/voice-to-text-conversion-with-audiopen-the-ultimate-web-app). UNVERIFIED for 2026.
- Developer said in an App Store reply that the AudioPen iOS keyboard has unlimited local transcriptions even on free: [AppsHunter review mirror](https://appshunter.io/ios/app/audiopen-ai-voice-to-text/id6502638001/reviews). UNVERIFIED.

**Letterly**
- Site says "Try it. It's free," "Letterly has no limits on recordings or AI rewrites," recordings up to 90 minutes, Zapier and webhooks, Apple Watch, widgets and Shortcuts. It does not say which of these apply to free users or list prices: [letterly.app](https://letterly.app/)
- Third party pricing: $19.99/month, $79.99/year with 7 day trial, $199.99 lifetime: [ToolQuestor](https://toolquestor.com/vs/letterly-vs-audiopen). SaaSWorthy says there is no free plan: [SaaSWorthy](https://www.saasworthy.com/product/letterly-app/pricing). Conflicting; free terms UNVERIFIED.

**TalkNotes**
- Homepage shows a 7 day free trial, then paid ($19.97/month shown, plus a $16 tier). No permanent free tier: [talknotes.io](https://talknotes.io/)
- Dataconomy also describes trial then paid: [Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/tools/talknotes/). Comparateur IA lists "free / from $7/month": [Comparateur IA](https://comparateur-ia.com/en/reviews/talknotes). The vendor page wins; treat as no free tier.

**Whisper Memos**
- Pricing page: $9.99/month or $69.99/year; "free to try" with no free plan described. Unlimited memos, up to 90 minutes each, Zapier routing, Apple Watch app with complication, Action Button, Back Tap and Siri start: [whispermemos.com pricing](https://whispermemos.com/landing/pricing)
- Third party listings conflict ("Free forever" vs "Free version unavailable"): [The Daily Workflow](https://thedailyworkflow.com/service/Whisper%20Memos); [FitGap](https://us.fitgap.com/products/060694/whisper-memos). Treat as paid with a trial.

**Superwhisper (iOS)**
- App is free with in-app purchases: [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6471464415)
- Official plans doc: free tier has basic transcription with limited cloud voice models, no custom modes, custom vocabulary or local models; new users get a 3,000 word Pro trial: [superwhisper docs: plans](https://superwhisper.com/docs/billing/plans.md)
- Third parties instead describe unlimited local small-model dictation and up to 3 custom modes on free: [Voibe](https://www.getvoibe.com/resources/superwhisper-pricing/); [BossAI](https://bossai.tech/blog/superwhisper-review). Conflicts with official doc. Pro $8.49/month, $84.99/year, $249.99 lifetime: [Voibe](https://www.getvoibe.com/resources/superwhisper-pricing/)
- Superwhisper is a dictation keyboard/tool, not a note library. Its free tier is not a capture-and-organize system.

**Tana**
- Free plan: "500 AI credits every month," renewing monthly, roughly enough for about 100 minutes of meeting transcription; mobile voice memos save as audio when credits run out and can be transcribed later; 0.5GB storage, 5MB per file; default AI model only; Input API integrations reserved for paid: [Tana blog: AI credits in Tana Free](https://outliner.tana.inc/blog/introducing-ai-credits-in-tana-free)

**Mem**

**Reflect**

**Otter**

### Inferences
- The Claude reachability goal and the zero budget goal conflict for every hosted app: the only first party Claude connector found (Voicenotes MCP) is Pro only. Free routes to Claude are manual: share sheet or copy into a file Claude can read.
- Apple Notes plus built-in transcription is the zero cost, zero limit baseline; it lacks AI restructuring unless the phone has Apple Intelligence.

### Gaps
- Official free tier export rules for Voicenotes, Letterly and AudioPen were not stated on their pages.
- Otter, Reflect, Mem and Notion official pricing pages were not fetched; figures above are third party.
- Exact Tana credits per voice memo minute unknown.

## Which free tiers survive 3 to 10 short memos a day long term?

### Takeaway
Cleft Basic, Voicenotes Basic and Apple Notes/Voice Memos survive daily use indefinitely. Tana Free probably does. AudioPen free does not if the 10 note storage cap is real; Notion's 20 lifetime AI responses do not; Otter is sized for meetings and works but does no idea structuring.

### Cited Findings
- Voicenotes 100 min/week covers about 14 min/day; 10 memos of 1 to 1.5 minutes fits, but only the last 30 days stay visible: [Voicenotes help](https://help.voicenotes.com/en/articles/15389129-pricing-and-billing)
- Cleft Basic caps length (5 min) not count, and transcribes on-device: [cleftnotes.com/pricing](https://cleftnotes.com/pricing)
- Tana's 500 credits ≈ 100 minutes of meeting transcription per month: [Tana blog](https://outliner.tana.inc/blog/introducing-ai-credits-in-tana-free)
- Otter 300 min/month: [Sonix](https://sonix.ai/resources/otter-ai-review-pricing/)
- AudioPen free reported 10 stored notes: [TechLearning](https://www.techlearning.com/how-to/audiopen-how-to-use-it-to-teach) (UNVERIFIED)
- Notion 20 total AI responses: [Fireflies](https://fireflies.ai/blog/notion-ai-meeting-notes)
- Mem free reported at 25 notes/month (July 2026, may have changed): [usagepricing.com](https://usagepricing.com/blueprint/activity/mem-2026-07-14-launch)

### Inferences
- Arithmetic: 10 memos x 1 min x 30 days = 300 min/month. That fits Voicenotes (about 430 min/month) and Otter (300 min/month, at the edge) but exceeds Tana's roughly 100 min/month; at 3 memos a day (about 90 min/month) Tana fits.
- Cleft Basic is the strongest fit on paper: on-device transcription means no minute meter, structured AI output is included, and share sheet export lets notes land in Files or Obsidian manually. Whether its structuring AI runs on-device or in the cloud on Basic was not confirmed.
- Mem at 25 notes/month would allow under one memo a day.

### Gaps
- No Reddit long-term usage reports were checked for Cleft or Voicenotes free tiers.

## Fully free or open source on-device options that save to Files or Obsidian

### Takeaway
WhisperBoard is free on the App Store and runs Whisper on-device; Obsidian community plugins Voice Scribe and Offline Whisper claim local Whisper transcription without API keys, but iOS recording support needs checking. Apple's own Notes/Voice Memos transcription is the simplest free on-device path.

### Cited Findings
- WhisperBoard: App Store listing says "Absolutely Free," iPhone, iPad and Mac, iOS 16+: [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1661442906). Open source repo by Saik0s: [GitHub mirror](https://fr.github.com/Saik0s/Whisperboard); described as fully free in [iphone-ticker.de](https://www.iphone-ticker.de/geheimtipp-whisperboard-gaenzlich-kostenfreie-sprache-zu-text-app-254839/). Obsidian export not mentioned.
- Aiko (Sindre Sorhus) runs Whisper locally on iPhone and Mac via whisper.cpp; sources disagree on whether it is free (one German article lists €29): [ifun.de](https://www.ifun.de/aiko-neue-und-kostenlose-transkriptions-app-fuer-den-mac-205461/); [GitHub discussion](https://github.com/openai/whisper/discussions/1300). Price UNVERIFIED.
- OpenWhispr claims free iPhone on-device transcription with no usage limit: [openwhispr.com](https://openwhispr.com/use-cases/iphone). Open source status and Obsidian support not stated.
- Obsidian plugin Voice Scribe: records and transcribes on-device with Whisper, no API keys, models cached in the app on mobile: [Obsidian community](https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/voice-scribe)
- Obsidian plugin Offline Whisper: MIT licensed, desktop via whisper.cpp and mobile via transformers.js/WebAssembly; recording on iOS not stated: [Obsidian community](https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/offline-whisper)
- Other Obsidian plugins (Whisper by nikdanilov, Voice MD) record on mobile but send audio to paid APIs such as OpenAI: [Whisper plugin](https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/whisper); [Voice MD](https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/voice-md)
- DictaWiz: on-device Whisper/Parakeet with direct Obsidian export; free only during TestFlight beta, otherwise one time paid: [Obsidian forum](https://forum.obsidian.md/t/ios-voice-keyboard-recording-transcription-with-obsidian-export/112299)

### Inferences
- A fully free path to Claude: record in Apple Notes or WhisperBoard, then share the transcript into an Obsidian vault (or iCloud Drive folder) that Claude Code or Claude Desktop can read as files. Organizing then happens on the Claude side, not the phone.
- Cleft Basic's share sheet plus auto-copy gets close to the same without Obsidian; its automatic Obsidian Local Sync is Plus only.

### Gaps
- Whether Voice Scribe actually records on iOS Obsidian was not tested or documented in sources found.
- WhisperBoard export formats not confirmed.

## Lock screen, Action Button, Siri and Apple Watch support

### Takeaway
Hardware shortcut support is best documented for paid apps. Among free options, Cleft Basic lists widgets plus Siri and Shortcuts; any app or Apple Voice Memos can be bound to the Action Button via Shortcuts, but per-app Watch support on free tiers is mostly unconfirmed.

### Cited Findings
- Cleft Basic: widgets, Siri and Shortcuts included; Lock Screen and Action Button (iPhone 15 Pro and later) documented, tier not confirmed: [cleftnotes.com/pricing](https://cleftnotes.com/pricing)
- Voicenotes: watchOS app listed; tier rules not stated: [voicenotes.com/pricing](https://voicenotes.com/pricing)
- Whisper Memos (paid): Apple Watch app with complication, Action Button, Back Tap, Siri: [whispermemos.com pricing](https://whispermemos.com/landing/pricing)
- Letterly: Apple Watch, widgets, Shortcuts; Action Button not mentioned; tier not stated: [letterly.app](https://letterly.app/)
- Tana has an iOS app with voice memos: [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/om/app/tana-notes-ai-voice-memos/id6499428869); Watch, widgets not confirmed.

### Inferences
- Because the Action Button can run any Shortcut, an app exposing Siri/Shortcuts actions (Cleft, Letterly, Apple Voice Memos) is effectively Action Button capable even if the vendor does not advertise it.

### Gaps
- Apple Watch support on free tiers for Voicenotes, Cleft, Tana, AudioPen and Otter not confirmed.
- No Lock Screen widget details found for AudioPen, Otter, Tana or WhisperBoard.
