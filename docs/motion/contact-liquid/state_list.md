# Liquid Contact button: state list

The render drives the scripted pointer (DEMO in the engine, white dot) through every state. 9.0 s loop.
Times are seconds in the render; each line is a cut the review strips check.

- 0.00 s: rest, pointer above the button. Two crisp Figma pills, separate outlines, no colour.
- 0.90 s: pointer just above the circle's top edge. The body dents and the hidden colour shows in it.
- 1.30 s: pointer crosses into the button. The circle starts pulling left and tips; the melt fades in.
- 2.00 s: pointer inside the label. The outline bulges out over the pointer, top and bottom.
- 2.60 s: pointer near the label's bottom right. The bulge travels with it.
- 3.00 s: pointer leaves through the bottom edge. The edge catches.
- 3.35 s: pointer below and moving away. The edge is stretched into a point after it.
- 4.10 s: pointer past the let go distance. The edge snaps back with a wobble; the circle returns.
- 5.00 s: pointer just below the label. A dent with the hidden colour rises from the bottom edge.
- 5.60 s: press inside. Both shapes squeeze in.
- 6.50 s: release. They spring back out.
- 7.10 s: pointer leaves through the right end. The right end stretches after it.
- 8.00 s: pointer far right. Everything settles.
- 8.50 s: pointer back at its start point above the button and held still.
- 9.00 s: rest again; the last frame matches the first (loop seam).
