# Home load: state list

One pass, 2.0 s from navigation start, at 1440 by 900. Times are seconds from navigation start; each line
is a cut the review strips check. The phone version (402 by 874) follows the same times on the vertical
CHDWCK.

- 0.00 s: black page. Nav in place (logo, Product Designer, About, Work, Contact me). Lede, location and
  wordmark hidden.
- 0.90 s: still the black hold, nav only.
- 1.00 s: the lede and Chicago with Looking to relocate start to fade in together. Phone: the Contact
  button with them.
- 1.20 s: CHD starts to rise from 0.83 of its height below its place, at opacity 0. Lede and location
  about half in.
- 1.40 s: lede and location fully in. CHD most of the way up and still accelerating (ease in).
- 1.60 s: CHD lands in place at full opacity. WCK starts its rise from below.
- 1.75 s: WCK most of the way up, slowing (ease out).
- 2.00 s: WCK lands. The hero matches the shipped site exactly: the wordmark is the original image again.
- After: scroll and everything below the hero behave as Home motion has them. Scrolling during the load
  does not stop or restart it.
- Replay (Home load panel): the hero goes back to the 0.00 s state and the pass runs again from the
  1.00 s cut after the hold.
