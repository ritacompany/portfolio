# Capacity card cursor demo: state list

Plays once, 5.2 s. Frame is the card, 616 by 596 (the @2x export). Times are when each state begins; review
strips are taken around each.

- 0.00 s: the export as is. 6 hours, about 4 Action Items, both buttons at rest, no cursor
- 0.35 s: cursor enters from the bottom right edge, arcs toward the right arrow and lands on its lower right
- 1.05 s: cursor over the right arrow: hover outline
- 1.42 s: press: right arrow pressed, cursor dips
- 1.56 s: release: hover again, 7 hours, about 4
- 1.92 s: press: right arrow pressed
- 2.06 s: release: 8 hours, about 5
- 2.40 s: cursor leaves the right arrow (back to rest) and travels left, arcing under the number
- 2.85 s: cursor over the left arrow: hover outline
- 3.36 s: press: left arrow pressed
- 3.50 s: release: 7 hours, about 4
- 3.90 s: cursor leaves the left arrow (rest) and drifts out through the left edge, above the Action Items line
- 4.80 s: cursor gone
- 4.80 s to 5.20 s: hold on 7 hours, about 4, both buttons at rest
