# Chat phone turn: state list

1. The phone's top comes into view at the frame 1 tilt, the paragraph under it as in frame 1, visible.
2. Approach: the paragraph scrolls with the page. The phone falls behind the scroll (it rises slower than the
   page) while it turns to face on and drifts 90 px left, so the paragraph catches up with it.
3. They line up in the frame 2 layout with the phone in the middle of the screen.
4. From there both scroll on together. No pin anywhere.
Reduced motion: frame 2 as a still.
Narrow screens (under 768): phone centred and sized to the screen, paragraph under it, the turn runs from
the phone entering to it reaching mid screen.
