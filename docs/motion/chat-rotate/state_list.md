# Chat phone turn: state list

Agreed with Chadwick, 28 Oct (round 7). He saw two versions on the page's smooth scroll and picked
"phone slows down", with a longer hold. Numbers are for a 1440 by 900 screen.

The "Interview insight 2" chip names the insight, so it belongs to the phone. In both Figma frames it sits
72 px above the top of the phone image, on the right edge. It rides with the phone the whole way and holds
with the pair (added 29 Oct, Chadwick).

The paragraph never moves on its own: it is part of the page. The phone, with its chip, is the only thing that moves. It
travels down through the page to the paragraph, then they lock. On screen, because the page is scrolling
up, that reads as the phone rising slower than everything around it.

1. Phone comes up with the page. The phone scrolls in at page speed, still at the frame 1 tilt. Its own
   motion starts once its centre reaches the bottom of the screen (half the phone in view).
2. Parallax. From there the phone rises slower than the page, slowing steadily to a stop, while it turns
   from the frame 1 tilt toward face on and slides 90 px left. The paragraph is plain text scrolling at page
   speed, never faded.
3. The paragraph shows up first. While the phone is still in the lower half of the screen the paragraph
   comes in at the bottom edge, about 424 px below the phone centre as in frame 1, and closes the gap.
4. They meet. The phone arrives at rest in the middle of the screen, no overshoot, and the paragraph
   reaches it. The turn is done and the layout is exactly frame 2: phone face on, paragraph to its left, its
   top 13 px above the phone centre. Scroll from step 2 to here: about 890 px.
5. Locked and held. The pair stays in place on the screen for one full screen of scroll (900 px), room to
   read the paragraph and look at the phone. Nothing moves during the hold.
6. Leave together. After the hold the pair scrolls off the top at page speed as one block, and the next
   section follows.

Reduced motion: frame 2 as a still, no turn, no parallax, no hold.
Narrow screens (under 768): phone centred and sized to the screen with the paragraph under it. It turns from
the same start point, the pair holds centred on the screen for one screen of scroll, then leaves.
