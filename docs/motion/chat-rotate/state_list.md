# Chat phone turn: state list

1. The phone's top comes into view: the turn starts, from the frame 1 tilt. The paragraph is under the phone,
   as in frame 1, visible from the start.
2. A little before the paragraph reaches its hold spot the phone starts lagging the scroll.
3. Hold (0.7 screen heights of scroll): the paragraph stays still. The phone comes down to land beside it,
   finishing the turn face on and drifting 90 px left, in the frame 2 layout.
4. After the hold: frame 2. Both scroll on together.
Reduced motion: frame 2 as a still, no hold.
Narrow screens (under 768): phone centred and sized to the screen, paragraph under it, the turn runs from
the phone entering to it reaching mid screen, no hold.
