# Growth Journey image, style guide

Reference: his Growth Journey screen, Figma file IarvWaqjIwrZ2afQmMxKm6, block 4809:27610. Exported from two
copies placed beside his layout (4828:15927 base, 4828:16185 lit), scaled 3x, real dates from
reference/cs2/versions/growth-journey.md. His original is untouched.

Take:
- The screen's own connector between the Pathway cards. The fill draws over those four bars in his accent
  (57,241,69), left to right, the way his completed bars already read.
- His Current card border colour (9,201,22) for the journey tiles once lit, and his accent for the matching dates.
- Every card's target and pace visible from the first frame.

Do not take:
- No cursor, no line drawn up to the tiles, no number travelling (ruled out rounds 1 and 2).
- No glow, no scale on cards, no fade of the whole screen.
