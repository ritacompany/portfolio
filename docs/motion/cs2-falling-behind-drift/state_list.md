# CS2 Falling behind, Drift: state list

Loop of 8 s. The field drifts the whole time and loops on its own; everything else fades in and out.

- 0.00 s: field only
- 0.00 s to 0.50 s: labels and rows fade in. This week: open item on top, one finished, one open below it. Next week: 3 items.
- 0.90 s: the third item in This week gets its check and dims
- 1.70 s: the magenta ROLLOVER chip appears on the top item
- 2.50 s: the item lifts
- 2.70 s: it travels right into the top of Next week; Next week steps down, its last item makes way
- 3.75 s: it lands; the chip goes, the green outline draws around it and the ring turns green
- 4.00 s: This week closes the gap
- 4.60 s to 6.90 s: hold
- 6.90 s: labels and rows fade out
- 7.40 s to 8.00 s: field only, back to the first frame
