# CS2 Falling behind, Rebuild: style guide

Concept, his pick of three (4 Nov): the open item is tagged for rollover, breaks into pixel blocks that
stream right, and reassembles in next week as the active item. The particles are the item, never decoration.

Reference: his CS2 portfolio frame (tiles in .cs2-canvas/img/tiles, not in git): the Action Hub list and
its glitch backdrop. Take the active item look, the mono uppercase chips and the glitch streak colours.
Do not take the browser chrome, the copy or the backdrop image itself.

- Layout: This week on the left, Next week on the right, 3 items each (his note). Frame 720 by 680.
- Rows: dark cards #141416, border #26262a, title and meta bars as in direction A. Finished rows dim to 40 percent.
- Active item, sampled from his frame (ESTIMATE, from an export): outline #3da342, ring #09c916, ring stroke heavier than the plain one.
- Rollover chip: mono uppercase like his USER TESTING chips, magenta. Hex #e31ecf sampled from the brand glitch streaks (ESTIMATE). Chip text ROLLOVER is a placeholder: label copy he has not approved.
- Pixel blocks: the card cut into a grid of small squares; while travelling, some take the glitch colours magenta #e31ecf, cyan #3ad7e8 and green #09c916, as short horizontal streaks. Seeded, never Math.random.
- Next week keeps its size: its last item makes way as the carried one rebuilds.
- Banned: glow, a particle field in the background, bursts outward, gradients, any new copy beyond the placeholder chip.
