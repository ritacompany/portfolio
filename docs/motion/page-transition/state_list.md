# Page change: state list

Clip: a real browser recording of the built site (scripts/record-browser.mjs with
docs/motion/page-transition/record.json, filmed ten times slower and sped back up, so it runs in real
time with every frame). Home, scroll to the Mondai card, click it; click Framework CS; back; back.
Times are clip seconds at 1440 by 900; the 390 by 844 clip lands within 0.02 s of them.

- 1.50 s: click the Mondai card on Home. Home fully visible.
- 1.70 s: Home gone into the page colour. Nothing of Home is left; the collection is starting to show.
- 1.90 s: the fade is done. The collection's own entrance has not started its text yet.
- 2.30 s: the Mondai wordmark and the rows are part way through their 1.4 s fade.
- 4.12 s: click Framework CS on the collection.
- 4.32 s: the collection gone into the page colour.
- 4.52 s: the fade is done; the framework page shows only the page colour and the nav.
- 4.90 s: the Framework CS title is fading in on its own entrance.
- 7.14 s: browser back from the framework page.
- 7.34 s: the framework page gone; the collection arrives and its entrance plays again.
- 9.54 s: browser back from the collection to Home.
- 9.74 s: Home is back at full, where it was left (scrolled to the Mondai card).

Cuts: the four clicks and backs (1.50, 4.12, 7.14, 9.54). No loop.
