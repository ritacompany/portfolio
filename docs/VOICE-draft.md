## How he writes

1. **The user is the subject.** He explains the product from the user's side and calls them the user.
   "Setting a preferred time is essentially the user telling the product their own availability."
   "People need to understand how their current pathway connects to their entire growth journey."
2. **He says what a thing was designed to do.** "This page was designed to offer a zoomed out look of the
   user's entire journey." "I wanted to make sure that the framework I designed allowed the user the
   freedom they needed to reschedule their action items." I appears for his intent and his decisions.
3. **The reason rides inside the sentence.** Because, so and meaning join the decision to its reason in
   one longer sentence instead of two short ones. "The list view is still accessible because calendar sync
   is optional and the framework has to work with or without it." "The product uses one way calendar
   integration, meaning Mondai is able to read the user's calendar but..."
4. **A worked example with real numbers.** "For instance, if the user selected a range from 8 a.m. to
   9 p.m, the earliest time they would see on their calendar would be 8 a.m." "Suzie's capacity is set to
   6 hours per week. This week, she has 3 action items, and last week, she had 4."
5. **Soft, honest qualifiers.** essentially, more of a suggestion, entirely okay, easily. He says how firm
   a rule is instead of making everything sound absolute.
6. **Scope said plainly.** "Post MVP, we will revisit this process." What shipped and what waits are both
   stated without spin.
7. **He ends on the last fact.** No closing line that sums up or lands a point.

## What reads as AI next to his writing

- Clipped declaratives in a row and a short kicker at the end: "There is no streak."
- Things doing human jobs: "it also carries the job of keeping them going."
- Someone, a person and people rotated where he would write the user.
- Lists of features with no reason attached: "Suggested times, the calendar and the start of every new week
  all happen on this screen."
- Tidy contrasts and paired clauses built for rhythm instead of explanation.
- Referring to parts of the product the page has not introduced yet.

## What gets cleaned up and what stays

because and so joins. Hold the house rules: no dashes, no Oxford commas, sentence case.
