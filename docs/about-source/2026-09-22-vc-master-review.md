## Correction after Chadwick's review

The initial review treated the hero, the first two superpower lines and the psychology paragraph as repeated claims about one ability. That was wrong. It collapsed pattern recognition, sensitivity to what affects an outcome and systems thinking into one category. It also proposed editing the superpowers statement, star and three lines, which Chadwick has now explicitly placed outside the editing scope. The earlier recommendation to change the star's wording is withdrawn for this review despite a discrepancy with an older locked attribution line.
