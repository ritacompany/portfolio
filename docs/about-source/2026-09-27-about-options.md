## 1. Start with the problem

In my work, I'm drawn to the problem underneath the problem, the structural part that a surface request is usually pointing to.

I studied psychology before I studied design, and the thing that stuck was that a cause and an effect are never a fixed pair. Whether two variables stay connected, flip or quietly stop mattering changes depending on what else is in the room.

I rely on familiar patterns where they make a product easier to use. When evidence or a business need points toward a less conventional design, I weigh the benefit against any extra effort it asks of people.

Most of what I know about my own work came from someone else saying it out loud first. So I try to keep a room where no voice feels less than another.
