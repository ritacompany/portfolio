## Analysis for internal use

### What Chadwick says

- Usability matters to him. He often begins by looking at competitors and how people currently use similar products, then establishes a familiar foundation.
- That foundation gives him boundaries for trying something different when evidence, business needs or the value of visual impact justify it.
- He enjoys both useful product systems and more visually exploratory design. He expects personal projects to remain a place to explore interests a job may not satisfy.
- He prefers clear expectations and guidelines. He identifies adapting a process that works for him to the nuance of a specific situation as an unresolved struggle.
- Design extends beyond interface work in his self-description. His statements about always loving it and about his work habits are present feelings and expectations, not evidence of permanent traits.

### Interpretations to test, not settled claims

- His note describes a specific kind of judgment: he looks at what people already know so he can decide where familiarity helps and where a different approach may serve the situation better. The foundation gives him a way to assess creative departures, rather than restricting him to one design style.
- He considers several kinds of value at once: ease of use, the product's purpose, business needs and the possible effect of a more expressive visual choice. He does not describe one of these as a universal winner.
- Familiar patterns seem to serve as a reference point, not an automatic answer. The note does not yet show how he decides which conventions actually help users and which merely reflect what competitors do.
- Clear expectations appear to help him get oriented and do this kind of judgment. He also names adapting a familiar process to nuanced situations as an area he is still working through. That struggle is one part of the note, not its main conclusion.
- The different kinds of design he enjoys do not necessarily need equal expression in every role. His note suggests he can keep exploring through self-directed work, but it does not establish what mix he wants from a job.
