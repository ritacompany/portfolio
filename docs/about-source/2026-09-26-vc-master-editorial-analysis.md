## The missing idea

The page does not yet show Chadwick's decision rule: he establishes what users can rely on, then decides when evidence, business needs or visual impact justify departing from it. His notes describe this as a way of weighing competing considerations in context.

## Ordinary UX practice in the notes

- Studying competitors and how people currently use similar products.
- Looking for gaps, missing considerations and overlooked issues.
- Using UX practices and psychology to inform design.
- Building toward familiar, intuitive experiences.

These are strong practices, but hiring managers will not read them as distinctive on their own. Chadwick explicitly identifies research and finding overlooked issues as his top priority, with UX and psychology as a second layer.

## Specific design judgment in the notes

- Familiar patterns are a baseline and boundary, not a default answer. Chadwick says the foundation lets him test something different when there is a reason.
- He weighs a departure against its usability cost. Evidence, a business need or the effect of a visual choice can make a less conventional choice worth making.
- Visual expression is neither automatically valuable nor automatically secondary. Its value depends on the situation.
- Practice context can change his approach. His notes specifically ask that adaptability be understood across different practice contexts.

This is an inference from how he describes making choices, not a claim he stated word for word.

## What the current VC Master communicates

### Hero

It presents Chadwick as a Chicago-based product designer who notices patterns in noisy situations. It provides a memorable lens but does not explain what he does with those patterns.

### Superpowers

It names an eye for what quietly moves an outcome, pattern recognition and interpersonal energy. Chadwick clarified that these are related to systems thinking but are not interchangeable with it. The section names strengths, it does not explain decision-making.

### About opener and psychology paragraph

They communicate that he looks beneath surface requests and understands causes as dependent on context. This conveys systems thinking, but stops before showing how that understanding changes a design choice.

### Verbal processor passage

It communicates a value for conversation and equitable collaboration. Chadwick says its intended meaning is stronger: he understands that people think and work differently, then adapts how he works with them. The current wording reads mainly as a preference for collaborative environments.

### Current role

It presents real responsibility and influence over product direction. It grounds the portrait in a role but does not add the missing decision rule.

## Editorial conclusion

The page currently portrays a perceptive, contextual and collaborative designer. It does not yet show the central thing the notes reveal: how he decides what should remain familiar and where a contextual departure earns its cost.
