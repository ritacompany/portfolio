Overview

In my work, I'm drawn to the problem underneath the problem, the structural part that a surface request is usually pointing to.

I studied psychology before I studied design, and the thing that stuck was that a cause and an effect are never a fixed pair. Whether two variables stay connected, flip or quietly stop mattering changes depending on what else is in the room.

No problem is the same, and neither is one person. I’m a verbal processor that thrives in environments that foster collaboration. Most of what I know about my own work came from someone else saying it out loud first. So I try to keep a room where no voice feels less than another.

Current role

I lead design at Mondai, a tech career tool that the industry forgot to map. Two years, unpaid, one of four people deciding what it becomes. I met the founder when he was my mentor. Now I'm one of the voices he argues with.
