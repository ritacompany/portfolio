Haven ending study, round two

Three ways to close the case study. Each opens with a plain "What happens next" at Heading/64 and avoids list rows, since lists on the Haven page are interactive. Everything inside the dark frames is the design. The boxed notes underneath are rationale only.

Option A · Bookend

What happens next

The case study stops at the Identity launch. Next I’d retest the revised flow with new participants, then run a study teens can join without a parent’s permission, then build Phase 2, Community.

A profile gives people information.
A conversation gives them trust.

FooterAll work

Rationale

Concept: close the story the way the page turned it. The gray band that introduced the solutions comes back once to deliver the lesson, so the case study ends on a bookend.

Plain header, one sentence of plan. Inverted pyramid. The next steps are written as a single sentence, so they read as a plan without looking like the page's interactive lists.

The band. Peak end rule and repetition. People remember the peak and the end of an experience. Repeating a pattern the reader has already seen signals a deliberate close, not another section.

Band runs into the footer. Closure. Nothing sits between the lesson and the end of the page.

Option C · Closing note

What happens next

Haven stops at the Identity launch, before anyone has used it at home. That’s where it gets proven. First I’d retest the new flow and see if people still change their pick. Then I’d study teens directly, without needing a parent’s permission. And I’d measure less than usual, on purpose. For someone who isn’t out, a usage log is a record of who they are.

FooterAll work

Rationale

Concept: end in your own voice. A short first person note at headline size, the way a good essay ends.

Two tones. Scannability. The bright sentences carry the plan, and the gray ones carry the reasoning. A skimmer reading only the bright lines still gets the whole ending.

Measure less, on purpose. Shows measurement judgment in a single line instead of a metrics block, which is the senior signal the research pointed to.

No visuals. Contrast with everything above it. After a page of screens and diagrams, a quiet block of type reads as the conclusion.
