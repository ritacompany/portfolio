About

I’m convinced that people would be happier if we encouraged one another to discover our superpowers. I’m lucky enough to have met some of the right people to help discover some of mine.

  • The eye to see what’s quietly moving the needle.
  • A proclivity for pattern recognition.
  • The kind of energy that pulls people in.

I studied psychology before I studied design, and the thing that stuck was that a cause and an effect are never a fixed pair. Whether two variables stay connected, flip or quietly stop mattering changes depending on what else is in the room.

I believe that now more than ever, product design requires the ability to always be two steps ahead. I design with systematic, future-focused intentionality. Building proactive, AI-scaleable solutions for the modern landscape.
