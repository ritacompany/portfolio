Haven pill tooltips

Two tooltips for the insight pills. Both behave as a tooltip should: they open on hover after a short delay and on keyboard focus, never on click, close on Escape or when the pointer leaves, and you can move the pointer onto them to keep reading. The only difference is how the box sits against the chip. Every pill is a real one from the page with its real text.

My pick is 1, because it is the Figma spec and its tuck already does the arrow's job: the box visibly comes out of one chip, so it cannot be read as belonging to its neighbour.

Option 1

Tucked behind the chip

The Figma box (4264:7197): left edges aligned, the box tucked 13px behind the chip so the chip's border sits on it, 28px of padding on the side away from the chip.

Opens on
Hover, after 300ms. Keyboard focus, at once. Once one is open, moving to the next chip opens it at once (no second wait).
Position
Above, left aligned, 13px under the chip. Flips below when there is no room above, with the deep padding moving to the bottom. Shifts left to stay 16px inside the right edge.
Arrow
None. The tuck connects box and chip.
Motion
Rises 10px out from behind the chip with a fade, 240ms expo out. Leaves in 120ms. Reduced motion keeps the fade only.
Closes on
Pointer leaving both chip and box, after 100ms. Blur. Escape.

Option 2

Detached with an arrow

The same box held 8px off the chip, with an arrow on the chip's centre. NN/g recommends the arrow when elements sit close together, and the chips here are 14px apart.

Both options

Keyboard, screen readers and phones

Keyboard
Each chip takes focus (tabindex 0) so the tooltip opens on focus. Escape closes it and focus stays on the chip.
Screen reader
The box has role tooltip and the chip points at it with aria-describedby, so the insight is read after the chip's label.
Phones
No tooltip. Touch screens have no hover, so the chips stay plain tags, as they are today. Every insight is already on the page in Key insights and the testing cards.
