Button and Small button states

Phase 2. Three options for hover, pressed and focus. Each row shows the states side by side; hover and press the "Try it" pair to feel them. Focus is the same 2 px white ring everywhere. Never shipped: the build leaves site/dev out.

A. Fill

The outline fills with Neutral/100 and the text and icon turn dark: icon pill first, label 60 ms behind. Pressed sinks to Neutral/300 and shrinks to 98 percent. The plainest and the most obviously clickable.
