# Case study rules

NOT loaded every turn. Read this only when working on a case study or on build/.
Kept out of the standing injection on purpose, because it does not apply to About,
hero or brand work and every line of it would otherwise compete for attention there.

## EVIDENCE
- Every claim traces back to something in `reference/`. If it does not trace, it does not ship.
- Mark what you are asserting: CONFIRMED, ESTIMATE, ASSUMED, INVENTED, PARAPHRASE.
  An unmarked number reads as invented.
- `reference/cs1/CS1-PRIMARY-EVIDENCE-VERBATIM.md` and `reference/cs1/02-EVIDENCE_1.md` are the
  clean record, reproduced exactly as said including typos. Quote out of them, never edit them
  to fit a draft. A hook blocks writes to both.
- Where a quote is marked paraphrase, get the real version before using it.

## SCOPE. Three case studies, three jobs. Never let them bleed.
- CS1 owns the operation: research ops, the volunteer design team, the systems he built.
- CS2 owns the framework: the Growth Journey, weekly scheduling, product AI strategy.
- CS3 owns the design system.
- Material belonging to another case study gets noted and left where it is, never absorbed.

## BUILD
- `build/` holds the Metalab layout rebuilds. `LEDGER.md` carries their state. Read it before
  touching anything in `build/`.
- Body copy in those files is Metalab scaffold text, sized so section heights are right.
  Not shippable. It gets replaced before publishing.

## COPY, case study specific
- No vanity metrics. Results precise and matched to the problem they answer.
- Behavioural principles go in captions or asides. Never "we used loss aversion" in the body.
