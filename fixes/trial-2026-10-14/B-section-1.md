Block 1, without a figure the reader misses: the picture of a fixed route with separate pieces sitting around it and nothing joining them. "Nothing said how to move through it" is an absence, and an absence is easier to see than to read. One figure fixes it: the route drawn as a row of five numbered stages joined left to right (time runs left to right, so the fixed order reads without a word), with the onboarding quiz and the calendar connection as loose boxes beside it and no lines to the route or to each other. Boxes contain, lines connect, so the missing lines are the point. The calendar box is drawn with a dashed edge because it was only planned. The three label lines become the annotations on those three things. Stage names stay off the figure, since no product term is needed before section 2. "A way to pick a career" stays in the body and gets no box. Nothing is still missing once the figure is there, so no second figure.

Block 2, without a figure the reader misses: nothing. The headline states the tension and the four questions are already the clearest form of what was unanswered. Drawing them would only restate them. The block stays text only, with the four questions set as a numbered list under the body.

Block 3, without a figure the reader misses: what each product actually does at the moment that matters, so "each solved half" stays an assertion. The reader has to see the three side by side, each kept for one thing and left for another, to believe none of them held both. One figure fixes it: three equal panels in one frame, one per product, each showing the product's own moment (a fixed course schedule, a streak that resets after a missed day, a due list with no sense of where it leads) with two annotations on it, kept and left, taken from the label lines. Equal size and the same two annotations in each panel keep them parallel, so the only thing that changes is the product. The label lines fold into those annotations and leave the body. The closing paragraph about the unnamed career products stays body only, since there is nothing to show for products the copy does not name. Nothing is still missing, so no second figure.
